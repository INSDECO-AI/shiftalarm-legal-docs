Buku la wogwiritsa ntchito ShiftAlarm

Mtundu 1. Tsiku: 2026-06-23.

1. Kuyambitsa koyamba

Tsegulani pulogalamuyi, sankhani chilankhulo chofunika, yang’anani zilolezo ndi kukonza kudalirika kwa ma alamu. Pa zida zina mungafunikenso kulola ntchito yakumbuyo ndi kuchotsa zoletsa za batire.

2. Ma alamu

Pangani ma alamu wamba, ma alamu obwereza malinga ndi masiku, ma alamu a tsiku lenileni ndi ma alamu olumikizidwa ndi sifiti. Pa ma alamu ofunika, yang’anani zidziwitso, ma alamu a nthawi yeniyeni ndi zoletsa za batire.

3. Ma alamu a malo

Pangani alamu ya malo pa mapu mwa kukanikiza kwa nthawi yayitali. Kuti ikhale yodalirika kwambiri, gwiritsani ntchito utali wa 800–1000 m ndipo lolani malo olondola ndi malo akumbuyo.

4. Masifiti

Pangani ndandanda ya masifiti yogwira ntchito. Ma alamu olumikizidwa ndi sifiti ndi ma widget a kalendala amagwiritsa ntchito ndandanda yogwira ntchito ndipo amawerengera kusintha kwa masiku.

5. Zolemba ndi zikumbutso

Gwiritsani ntchito zolemba za malemba, mindandanda, zochitika, zikumbutso, zolemba za mawu ndi kusintha mawu kukhala malemba. Mitundu ya kuzindikira mawu popanda intaneti ingatsitsidwe mu Zokonda.

6. Ma widget

Onjezani Today Widget kapena Calendar Widget pa sikirini yayikulu ya Android. Ma widget amawonetsa chidziwitso chapafupi ndi kusintha zokha, ndipo alinso ndi batani losintha pamanja.

7. Zosunga

Zokonda zimalola kusunga zosunga pa foni kapena kugawana kudzera pa Android share sheet.

8. Kuzindikira mavuto

Ngati ntchito ina sikugwira bwino, tsegulani mbiri yoyenerera ya kuzindikira mavuto, ikopereni kapena itumizeni kwa wopanga limodzi ndi kufotokoza vuto.
