Gwaro remushandisi weShiftAlarm

Shanduro 1. Zuva: 2026-06-23.

1. Kuvhura kekutanga

Vhura app, sarudza mutauro unodiwa, tarisa mvumo uye gadzirisa kuvimbika kwemaaramu. Pane mimwe michina, ungangodawo kubvumira kushanda kumashure nekubvisa ganhuriro dzebhatiri.

2. Maaramu

Gadzira maaramu enguva dzose, maaramu anodzokorora nemazuva, maaramu ezuva rakatarwa nemaaramu akasungirirwa kushifiti. Pamaaramu akakosha, tarisa zviziviso, maaramu chaiwo neganhuriro dzebhatiri.

3. Maaramu enzvimbo

Gadzira aramu yenzvimbo pamepu nekudzvanya kwenguva refu. Kuti ishande zvakavimbika, shandisa radius ye800–1000 m uye bvumira nzvimbo chaiyo nenzvimbo yekumashure.

4. Mashifiti

Gadzira hurongwa hwemashifiti hunoshanda. Maaramu akasungirirwa kushifiti nemawidget ekarenda anoshandisa hurongwa hunoshanda uye anofunga nezvekushandurwa kwemazuva.

5. Zvinyorwa nezviyeuchidzo

Shandisa zvinyorwa zvemashoko, marondedzero, zviitiko, zviyeuchidzo, zvinyorwa zveodhiyo nezvinyorwa zvinobva muzwi. Mamodheru ekuziva mashoko ezwi asingadi internet anogona kudhaunirodhwa muZvirongwa.

6. Mawidget

Wedzera Today Widget kana Calendar Widget pachidzitiro chikuru cheAndroid. Mawidget anoratidza ruzivo rwuri pedyo uye anozvigadziridza, uye ane bhatani rekugadziridza nemaoko.

7. Makopi ekuchengetedza

Zvirongwa zvinobvumira kuchengetedza kopi pafoni kana kuigovera kuburikidza neAndroid share sheet.

8. Kuongorora

Kana rimwe basa risingashandi nemazvo, vhura rekodhi rekuongorora rinoenderana, rikopa kana ritumire kumugadziri pamwe netsananguro yedambudziko.
