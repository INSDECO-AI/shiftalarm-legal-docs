ShiftAlarm iapp inobatsira pakuronga kwemunhu mashifiti, maaramu, maaramu enzvimbo, zvinyorwa, zviyeuchidzo, chiverengeri chenguva, wachi yekumisa, mawidget, makopi ekuchengetedza nemamwe mabasa anoenderana. App haisi sisitimu inovimbiswa yekuchengeteka, yekuzivisa zvekukurumidzira, yekutarisa zvokurapa, yevashandi, yekuverenga mari kana yemihoro.

1. App inopiwa “sezvairi”

App inopiwa “sezvairi” uye “sezvainowanikwa”. Mugadziri anotora matanho anoyenderana kuchengetedza kugadzikana kweapp, asi haavimbisi kuti mabasa ose achashanda pasina zvikanganiso, kunonoka, zviitiko zvinopotswa, kusaenderana, kurasika kwedata kana ganhuriro pamudziyo wese, shanduro yeAndroid, firmware yemugadziri nemamiriro ose ekushandisa.

2. Hapana vimbiso yakazara yekuendeswa

Maaramu, maaramu akasungirirwa kushifiti, maaramu enzvimbo, zviverengeri zvenguva, zviyeuchidzo, zviziviso nemawidget zvinoenderana neAndroid, mugadziri wemudziyo, mvumo, nguva yesisitimu yakarurama, nzira yekuchengetedza bhatiri, ganhuriro dzebasa rekumashure, mamiriro ebhatiri, Do Not Disturb, zvirongwa zvezviziviso, kutangazve, zvigadziriso zveOS, kuvhara app nemaoko, zvikanganiso zvesisitimu nezviito zvemushandisi.

Kuendeswa kunogona kunonoka, kupotswa, kuitika panguva yakasiyana neyakatarisirwa, kuitika pasina ruzha kana chiziviso, kana kuganhurirwa nesistimu.

3. Zviitiko zvakakosha nekushandiswa kwakakomba

Usashandise ShiftAlarm senzira chete yekutarisa zviitiko zvakakomba. Pamashifiti akakosha, nzendo, maitiro ezvokurapa, bvunzo, ndege, nguva dzakatarwa nemutemo, zviitiko zvine kukosha kwemari nezvimwe zvinodiwa, shandisa zviyeuchidzo nezvimwe zvitariso zvakazvimirira.

App haifaniri kushandiswa sechikamu chemasisitimu umo kukanganisa kana kunonoka kungakuvadza upenyu, utano, kuchengeteka, pfuma, zvifambiso, midziyo yemaindasitiri, michina yezvokurapa, masisitimu ekuchengetedza kana kupindura kwezvekukurumidzira.

4. Maaramu enzvimbo nenzvimbo

Maaramu enzvimbo anoenderana neGPS, Wi-Fi, manetwork enhare, kururama kwenzvimbo, kushandiswa mumba, kuwanda kwezvivako, mamiriro ekunze, nzvimbo yekumashure, ganhuriro dzeAndroid, zvirongwa zvebhatiri, radius yegeofence, kunaka kwemepu nekuwanikwa kwemasevhisi ekunze.

Aramu yenzvimbo inogona kutanga kunonoka, kusatanga, kutanga zvisina kururama kana kuganhurirwa nesistimu. Maaramu enzvimbo haana kugadzirirwa kutevera vanhu muchivande, kutarisa vashandi, mabasa ekuchengetedza, zviziviso zvekukurumidzira kana mabasa umo kukanganisa kwenzvimbo kungakuvadza.

5. Mepu nekero

Mepu, kero, makodhineti nezvibuda zvereverse geocoding zvinogona kusakwana, kusakara, kusava zvakarurama kana kusawanikwa. Kero inowanikwa kubva kumakodhineti iruzivo runobatsira uye haisi humbowo hwenzvimbo hwakanyatsorurama zviri pamutemo.

6. Odhiyo nezvinyorwa zvinobva muzwi

Kuziva mashoko ezwi, kusanganisira kunyora pasina internet kana paindaneti, kunogona kuva nezvikanganiso, kusiyiwa kwezvimwe zvinhu, kana kuziva zvisina kururama mazita, nhamba, mazuva, mazwi ehunyanzvi, mitauro nemitsara. Mushandisi anofanira kuongorora ega marekodhi akakosha.

Marekodhi eodhiyo nezvinyorwa zvinobva muzwi hazvifaniri kushandiswa sesosi chete yeruzivo rwakakosha zviri pamutemo, rwezvokurapa, rwemari, rwevashandi kana rumwe rwakakomba.

7. Muhoro nekuverenga

Mabasa emuhoro, bhonasi, zvinobviswa, chirango, coefficient yeshifiti nemishumo anobatsira uye ndeeruzivo chete. Haasi zano rekuverenga mari, remutero, remutemo kana revashandi.

Mushandisi anofanira kuzviongororera kuverenga achienzanisa nemagwaro epamutemo emushandirwi, zvibvumirano zvebasa, mapayslip, mitemo yemitero nemutemo unoshanda.

8. Makopi ekuchengetedza nekurasika kwedata

Mugadziri haavimbisi kuchengetedzwa kwedata kana mushandisi asina kugadzira kopi, akarasikirwa nefaira, akarichengeta munzvimbo isingawanikwi, adzima data reapp, abvisa zvirimo munzvimbo yekuchengetera, achinja mudziyo, ashandisa faira rakakuvadzwa, adzima mvumo inodiwa kana agovera faira nevamwe.

Makopi ekuchengetedza anogona kuva neruzivo rwemunhu. Mushandisi ane basa rekuachengeta, kuatumira, kuadzivirira nekuadzima.

Mugadziri haana mutoro wekuwanikwa, kururama, kuchengeteka, mitemo, shanduko, kutadza, ganhuriro kana zviito zvemasevhisi evamwe akasarudzwa nemushandisi kana anopiwa neoperating system.
