1. Controller and scope

INSDECO LLC is the personal-data controller for ShiftAlarm server features and support; its details appear below. Data inquiries: support@insdeco.ru. This Policy covers local processing, server accounts, trial and paid access, payments, devices, support and external services.

Core user content follows an offline-first model on the device. Server features receive the separate categories listed below; signing in does not automatically upload all notes, schedules, audio or geo-points to the developer.

The described configuration does not use advertising networks, advertising profiling, Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Crashlytics or AdMob. Account, access and security checks still take place and do not become anonymous merely because technical identifiers are used.

2. Data on the device

Depending on enabled features, the device may store:

  • language, theme, widget and app-behavior settings;
  • alarms, times, schedules, repeats, sounds, vibration, snooze and signal parameters;
  • shift schedules, templates, day overrides and salary, bonus and deduction calculations;
  • geo-points, coordinates, radii, addresses, saved places and geo-alarm states;
  • notes, lists, events, reminders, audio recordings and transcripts;
  • data for timer, stopwatch, world clocks, local history and diagnostics;
  • local session, device-binding and confirmed-access information;
  • user backup files, where the user creates them.

3. Data for server features

The following categories may be processed for trial activation, sign-in and accounts, access checks, purchases, renewal or deletion. Some are generated automatically when the app contacts the server, including trial activation without signing in.

  • email address, account identifier, email verification state, one-time code issuance and verification records, expiry and sign-in attempts;
  • installation identifier and the Android-provided ANDROID_ID used during trial activation to check prior use; device display name, app version and service timestamps;
  • linked-device identifiers and states, binding and last-activity dates, tokens and technical information for authenticated sessions;
  • access type, activation and end dates, verification results, trial state and paid entitlements;
  • order and payment identifiers, offer, amount, currency, time, status, refunds, renewal state and the identifier of a payment method saved by the payment service;
  • records and terms of enabling or disabling renewal, accepted document versions and service records of user actions;
  • account deletion requests, verification, outcome and refund processing;
  • IP address and other connection information, errors and security logs needed for service operation, rate limiting and abuse prevention.

4. Purposes and legal bases

Account creation and operation, sending a requested code, providing trial or paid access, device management and purchase fulfilment use data necessary to enter into and perform the corresponding agreement at the user’s initiative.

Payment records, lawful requests and mandatory retention rely on applicable legal obligations. Necessary technical information for preventing attacks, unauthorized access and repeated use of a limited trial is processed on an applicable lawful basis while respecting user rights.

Support inquiries and voluntarily sent materials are used to respond, handle the request and resolve the reported issue. They are not intended for advertising or advertising profiles.

Where a particular additional processing activity requires consent, its purpose and conditions are disclosed beforehand and separate verifiable consent is obtained. An Android permission, agreement acceptance or acknowledgment of this Policy does not replace that consent. Withdrawal does not stop processing supported by another continuing lawful basis.

5. Support inquiries

An inquiry to support@insdeco.ru may disclose the sender’s email address and signature, message text, attachments, screenshots, diagnostics, device, OS, app version and event time. Attachments are the user’s choice and should be reviewed for personal information and coordinates before sending.

Locating an account or payment may require proportionate email, date and transaction information. The controller does not ask users to send full card details, CVV, banking passwords, one-time banking codes or session tokens.

6. Permissions and system settings

The app uses permissions and system settings for notifications and exact alarms, foreground and background location, microphone, background activity and files selected by the user. Internet access supports server features and external downloads. The microphone is used for user-selected recording or recognition features.

Permissions can be revoked in Android settings, limiting the related features. Revocation does not erase previously created data, delete the server account or itself disable renewal.

7. Location and maps

Coordinates are used to select a place, save a geo-alarm, detect entry or exit and display an address. Location uses Android and device-provider system services; their network features also follow the relevant provider’s rules. Core geo-alarm settings remain on the device. The account and payment server does not receive a continuous user route to provide access.

Opening a map or looking up an address sends requests to OpenStreetMap/osmdroid, Nominatim, Android Geocoder or the relevant provider. The external service may receive the IP address, requested map areas, coordinates and request information. These are separate network requests, not wholly local operations.

Precise coordinates may appear in exported diagnostics or a backup. The user reviews them before voluntarily sharing the file.

8. Audio and speech recognition

Audio notes and transcripts are created through user action and normally remain local. Offline recognition uses a downloaded model; the model server receives standard network request information during download.

Online recognition may transmit audio and technical information to the system recognition service selected by Android, the manufacturer or the user. Processing also follows that provider’s rules. This transmission must be considered when choosing the online feature; the ShiftAlarm account server is not a repository of all audio notes.

The user decides what to record and respects other people’s rights. Unnecessary sensitive information, documents or recordings of third parties should not be sent to support.

9. Network downloads

Language packs, legal documents, help and models may be downloaded from GitHub or other sources identified in the app. Their servers receive the IP address and ordinary request information, including path and time. SHA-256 verification, where implemented, helps detect file changes but does not anonymize the network request.

10. Payments and renewal

For payments through YooKassa, payment credentials are entered with that payment service. The ShiftAlarm controller receives transaction information needed for access, records and refunds, and service information about the payment method available to it. Full card details, CVV and banking passwords are not sent to the ShiftAlarm account server for payment.

When renewal is separately enabled, the payment service saves the payment method and the controller uses its identifier and records the user’s choice, agreed conditions and later refusal. Refusal of further use of the method is accepted in the app and at support@insdeco.ru. Necessary records of past payments may remain after cancellation or account deletion.

A distribution store, such as RuStore, may process installation, update and store-account information under its own policy. If a purchase uses store billing, the store also processes information about that payment within its service. If YooKassa checkout is selected, YooKassa processes the payment. The actual payment service and its conditions are identified before purchase confirmation.

11. Recipients and outsourced processing

To provide the service, the controller uses a payment provider, hosting and email infrastructure, backup facilities and persons supporting operation and security to the necessary extent. Disclosures and outsourced processing are arranged on an applicable lawful basis and limited to the relevant purposes.

YooKassa, RuStore, GitHub, mapping services and system recognition services may also process information as independent providers under their own rules. Recipients for a particular operation can be clarified on request. Disclosure to competent authorities requires an applicable legal basis.

The controller does not sell user data to advertisers or use note content, audio or geo-points for advertising profiles.

12. Backups and your data

A user backup may contain schedules, alarms, notes, audio recordings, coordinates, settings and other selected data. The user creates the backup and chooses where to store it or with whom to share it. If email, cloud storage or another app is used, processing by that provider also follows its own rules.

The controller creates server backups to restore availability and protect the service. These may contain account, phone-binding, access, payment and service-operation data. Access is restricted to persons who need it for service recovery and protection. Backups are processed for those purposes within the retention periods described in section 13.

After account deletion, related information may temporarily remain in earlier server backups until they are deleted or updated within applicable retention limits. This does not mean the deleted account continues in ordinary use. Recovery takes previously completed deletions into account so restoring an old backup alone does not return account data to active use.

13. Retention periods and criteria

Local data remains until the user deletes it, clears app data or removes the corresponding file. Importing a backup can replace some data. User copies stored outside the app are managed separately.

Server account and active-access data is retained as needed to operate the account and fulfil obligations. After account deletion, processing of data without another basis must stop and that data must be deleted within statutory time limits.

Payment and accounting information is retained to the extent and for the periods mandatory for the corresponding records. For primary accounting documents, accounting registers and financial statements, Russian law requires retention for at least five years after the reporting year; certain categories may require longer retention. This is not general permission to retain all user data for five years or indefinitely.

Minimal deletion records remain while backups could restore the account or another lawful basis applies. A minimal technical indicator of trial use may remain while necessary and lawful to prevent repeat trials; it is not intended for advertising.

Login codes, session information and technical logs are retained as needed for sign-in verification, security and investigation of specific failures. Expiration limits use of a code or session; separate event records may remain where a corresponding purpose and basis exist. There is no single statutory maximum retention period for all such data.

Support and diagnostic materials remain until the inquiry is resolved and for a justified period needed to handle a subsequent claim or dispute, then are deleted or anonymized unless law requires otherwise. An inquiry without a fixed end date does not mean indefinite retention of every attachment.

Data is retained no longer than needed for its purpose or required by mandatory law. When the purpose ends and no other lawful basis remains, it is deleted or anonymized. By contacting support@insdeco.ru, the user may request an explanation of which data relating to them remains, why it is retained and the criteria used to determine its retention period.

14. Account deletion and local data

Deletion can be requested in the app account section or at support@insdeco.ru. Following the provided verification, linked access rights and renewal end and active bindings are removed; the request outcome and any refund follow the Terms.

Account deletion does not automatically erase all alarms, schedules, notes and files on the device or in the user’s external storage. Separate deletion and file-management actions apply. Signing in after completed deletion creates a new account and does not restore the former account data.

Immediate erasure of every related record is not promised: mandatory documents, unsettled transactions, lawful claims and minimal deletion records follow the grounds above. The user can request an explanation of remaining categories and retention grounds.

15. Data protection

Protection uses Android app isolation, permissions, access control, rate limits and the provided secure-connection mechanisms. Local authorization data is protected using Android Keystore in the implemented store. This does not mean the app encrypts every exported file or all device content.

On the developer’s side, operational access to server data, backups and support inquiries is held by one authorized person. Processing by infrastructure providers is limited to necessary purposes and corresponding grounds. No system is guaranteed to be absolutely secure; the controller takes protective measures and responds to incidents as required by law. Users protect their device, email and backup locations.

16. Where data is processed

Local content is on the user’s device. The main server account database and server backups are hosted in the Russian Federation. The email infrastructure used to send one-time codes and service messages is hosted in the Russian Federation.

Support inquiries are processed through the controller’s corporate email service. Hosting, email and backup-storage providers are engaged as needed for the relevant function on lawful grounds. Information about recipients and processing locations is provided on request where and to the extent required by applicable law.

Map, speech-recognition, network-file and app-store providers may process information in countries where their infrastructure is located. The recipient depends on the selected function and provider. Hosting the main account database in Russia does not by itself mean that all external app requests remain within Russia.

Where processing involves transferring data to another country, statutory requirements concerning grounds, notification and safeguards apply. This Policy is not blanket consent to every cross-border transfer and does not claim that every country provides equivalent protection.

17. Data rights and inquiries

The user can view, change and delete local data and manage permissions and copies. Where applicable law provides such rights, server-data requests may include processing information and a copy of data, correction, restriction, blocking or deletion, an objection, data portability or withdrawal of separately given consent.

Requests go to support@insdeco.ru. Account-ownership or identity checks must be proportionate; a passport copy or full payment details are not required by default. Responses and required actions follow applicable statutory time limits. The user may approach Roskomnadzor, another competent data protection authority or a court under applicable law, including any available right to seek remedies in their own country.

18. Children

The Russian 0+ rating concerns app content and does not remove specific requirements for processing children’s data or making purchases. The service is not intended to target collection of children’s information.

Where a child uses the app, a parent or other legal representative provides involvement and consent when required by applicable law, and supervises sharing of recordings, location, inquiries and payment methods. The controller’s data protection obligations and mandatory rights of the child and representative remain.

19. Policy changes

The document displays its version and date. New versions may arrive with the app or through its legal-document source. Material changes to purposes or data categories require an applicable lawful basis and, where needed, separate new consent. Publishing a new Policy alone does not authorize unrelated new purposes.
