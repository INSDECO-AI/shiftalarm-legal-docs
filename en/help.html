1. First launch and free mode

Choose a language, review the documents and configure permissions. The basic calendar and actions marked as free remain available in free mode. Some devices require additional background activity permission and removal of battery restrictions for alarms.

2. Trial period

The 30-day trial does not require account sign-in. Activation requires a server connection; the app shows the confirmed end date. The trial does not enable payment or renewal. Reinstalling or creating another account does not start another trial on the same device.

3. Account and phones

Open the account section, enter your email, review the policy and confirm sign-in with the six-digit email code. If delayed, check spam and the address; frequent repeat requests may be temporarily limited.

Linked devices shows the phone binding. An account has one active phone binding at a time. To change phones, use the provided explicitly confirmed transfer procedure; the previous active binding ends. Do not share sign-in codes.

4. Purchase and access restoration

The current distribution channel is RuStore. Sign in, check the phone binding and open access management. Before paying, check duration, the price in rubles, payment service and renewal choice. The developer’s current arrangement uses YooKassa. Wait for the payment status to be confirmed.

After reinstalling or changing phones, sign in with the original email and complete the access/device check. Transfer schedules, notes and audio separately using a user backup. Lifetime availability is limited to the first 10,000 paid purchases and is shown before payment.

If you are outside Russia, check support for your payment method in the YooKassa help linked below before paying. Available methods depend on checkout and the ShiftAlarm merchant setup; installing the app from RuStore does not by itself confirm that your card will be accepted. For an account in another currency, conversion and fees depend on the bank and selected method. Google Play is planned as a future channel without a set launch date.

5. Renewal and refunds

Renewal is available for 1-month and 1-year access only after a separate choice. The 2-year, 3-year and lifetime offers do not renew automatically. Disable it through access management; if the app is unavailable, email support@insdeco.ru. Uninstalling or signing out does not itself disable future charges.

Cancellation preserves the paid term. To end access early and request a refund, use the provided app action or support. Fixed-term access is refunded proportionately for the unused period. Lifetime access has an additional voluntary refund for requests within 7 days of payment; mandatory statutory grounds remain after that period. See the Terms for details.

6. Account deletion

Open account deletion, review the consequences and refund calculation, confirm email with the code and then separately confirm final deletion. You can also contact support@insdeco.ru.

Deletion ends linked paid access and renewal and removes device bindings. Alarms and other local content are managed separately. Signing in after completed deletion creates a new account. If the outcome is unknown, use Check deletion or support: closing the window does not cancel a request already accepted by the server.

7. Alarms and reliability

Create ordinary alarms, weekday repeats, date alarms and shift-linked alarms. Check notifications, exact alarms, sound, battery and background activity. After changing language, check labels and the selected sound. Test important alarms beforehand and use an independent backup reminder.

8. Geo-alarms

Create a geo-alarm with a long press on the map, choose entry or exit and set the radius. Current settings provide 200–5000 m with an initial 800 m radius. Allow precise and background location. Maps and addresses may contact external services; triggering depends on location accuracy and Android restrictions.

9. Shifts and calculations

Create and select an active schedule. Shift-linked alarms and related widgets use it and its day overrides. Check dates, times and shift changes before saving. Salary, bonus and deduction calculations are auxiliary; verify them against employer records.

10. Notes, audio and transcripts

Use text, lists, events, reminders and audio notes. Offline recognition models are downloaded in settings and then used on the device. Online recognition may send audio to the system provider; consider the recording’s content. Check important transcripts manually.

11. Widgets, timer and clocks

Add an available Today Widget or Calendar Widget from the Android home screen. Some capabilities depend on access type and system settings. Timer, stopwatch and world clocks are available in their app sections when access permits.

12. Transfer and backups

Save a user backup in settings and choose the required sections when restoring. Check that the backup contains needed content and audio files. Store it securely. Account sign-in does not replace this transfer. Keep the original copy until restored data has been checked.

13. Diagnostics and support

For a problem, open the relevant feature log, note the event time and describe what happened. Review diagnostics before sending, especially coordinates, names and attachments. Send selected materials to support@insdeco.ru with the app and Android versions and device model. Do not send full payment details, passwords or codes.

14. Documents and rights

The Terms describe payments, devices, termination and refunds. The Privacy policy describes data, external transfers, retention and data requests. The Disclaimer describes technical risks. This guide explains actions and does not remove mandatory user rights.
