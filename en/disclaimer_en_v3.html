1. Purpose of these limitations

ShiftAlarm is an auxiliary app for organizing personal time and related records. Its use must take account of device compatibility, settings and the technical limitations below.

Risk descriptions and expressions such as “as is” or “as available” do not remove mandatory quality requirements, the duty to provide paid access, statutory refund grounds or developer liability imposed by law. The developer takes reasonable steps to operate its features and address identified defects.

2. No absolute delivery guarantee

Alarms, shift-linked alarms, geo-alarms, timers, reminders, notifications and widgets depend on Android, the device manufacturer, permissions, correct system time, battery saving mode, background work restrictions, battery status, Do Not Disturb mode, notification settings, reboots, OS updates, manual app closing, system errors and user actions.

Delivery may be delayed, missed, occur at a different time than expected, occur without sound or notification, or be restricted by the system.

3. Important events and critical use

Do not use ShiftAlarm as the only control method for critical events. For important shifts, travel, medical procedures, exams, flights, legal deadlines, financially significant events and other important tasks, use additional independent reminders and checks.

The app must not be used as part of systems where an error or delay may cause harm to life, health, safety, property, transport, industrial equipment, medical devices, security systems or emergency response.

4. Geo-alarms and location

Geo-alarms depend on GPS, Wi-Fi, mobile networks, location accuracy, indoor use, building density, weather conditions, background location, Android system restrictions, battery settings, geofence radius, map quality and external service availability.

A geo-alarm may trigger late, not trigger, trigger inaccurately or be restricted by the system. Geo-alarms are not intended for hidden tracking of people, employee monitoring, security functions, emergency alerts or tasks where location error may cause harm.

5. Maps and addresses

Maps, addresses, coordinates and reverse geocoding results may be incomplete, outdated, inaccurate or unavailable. An address determined from coordinates is auxiliary information and is not a legally precise confirmation of location.

6. Audio and transcripts

Speech recognition, including offline and online transcription, may contain errors, omissions, incorrect recognition of names, numbers, dates, terms, languages and phrases. The user must check important records independently.

Audio recordings and transcripts must not be used as the only source of legally significant, medical, financial, HR or other critical information.

7. Salary and calculations

Salary, bonus, deduction, penalty, shift coefficient and report features are auxiliary and informational. They are not accounting, tax, legal or HR advice.

The user must independently verify calculations against official employer documents, employment contracts, payslips, tax rules and applicable law.

8. Backups and data loss

Local data may be lost through uninstalling, clearing data, device or file damage or an erroneous operation. Users should keep needed backups, check their usability and protect them against unauthorized access.

Signing in restores access rights confirmed by the server, but does not by itself recover lost local schedules, notes or audio. A damaged, incomplete or unavailable user backup may be unusable. This warning does not exclude liability for defects for which the developer is responsible by law.

9. Internet, server and external services

Maps, addresses, downloads, online recognition, sign-in, access checks, payments and refunds may depend on network connectivity and external providers. When the server is unavailable, some actions may be delayed or unavailable; offline access depends on its previously confirmed validity.

An unknown network result confirms neither success nor cancellation. Before paying or attempting deletion again, check the status or contact support. Closing a window does not cancel a request already accepted by the server.

An external provider failure can affect operation but does not automatically release the developer from its own obligations to the user. Provider rules apply alongside mandatory law.

10. User actions

The user is responsible for correctly setting up the app, schedules, alarms, geofences, permissions, notifications, battery, background work, backups, system time and for checking feature reliability on the user's device.

11. Liability and user rights

Liability follows applicable law, taking account of the breach, fault, causation and other legally relevant circumstances. Technical limitations mean no absolute trigger guarantee on every device; they do not provide a complete release from developer liability.

The Terms and this document do not exclude statutory claims concerning quality, refunds, data protection, compensation or other mandatory rights. Mandatory law prevails over a conflicting clause.
