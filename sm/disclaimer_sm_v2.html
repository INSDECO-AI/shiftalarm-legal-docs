Faʻamatalaga e Faʻatapulaʻa ai Noataga a ShiftAlarm

Version 2. Aso: 2026-06-29.

O ShiftAlarm o se polokalame fesoasoani mo le faʻatulagaina patino o sifi, faʻailo, faʻailo nofoaga, faʻamatalaga tusitusia, faʻamanatu, taimi faitau, uati taofi, widget, kopi faʻaleoleo ma galuega e fesootaʻi i ai. E lē o se faiga ua faʻamaonia mo le saogalemu, lapataiga o faʻalavelave faafuaseʻi, mataʻituina faʻafomaʻi, HR, tausitusi po o totogi o tagata faigaluega.

1. Ua tuuina atu le polokalame “e pei ona iai”

Ua tuuina atu le polokalame “e pei ona iai” ma “e pei ona maua”. E faia e le tagata atiaʻe taumafaiga talafeagai e faʻatumauina le mautu o le polokalame, ae e lē faʻamaonia o le a galue galuega uma e aunoa ma ni mea sese, tuai, mea e misia, lē fetaui, leiloa o faʻamatalaga po o tapulaa i masini uma, versions o Android, firmware a gaosi masini ma tulaga uma o le faʻaaogāina.

2. E leai se faʻamaoniga atoatoa o le auina atu

O faʻailo, faʻailo e fesootaʻi i sifi, faʻailo nofoaga, taimi faitau, faʻamanatu, faʻasilasilaga ma widget e faʻalagolago i Android, le gaosi masini, faʻatagaga, taimi saʻo o le faiga, auala e sefe ai le maa, tapulaa o galuega i tua, tulaga o le maa, Do Not Disturb, faʻatulagaga o faʻasilasilaga, toe amataina, faʻafouga o le OS, tapunia e le tagata le polokalame, mea sese o le faiga ma gaioiga a le tagata faʻaaogā.

E ono tuai, misia, tupu i se isi taimi nai lo le taimi na faʻamoemoeina, tupu e aunoa ma se leo po o se faʻasilasilaga, po o faʻatapulaʻaina e le faiga le auina atu.

3. Mea tāua ma le faʻaaogāina mataʻutia

Aua neʻi faʻaaogā ShiftAlarm o le auala e tasi e pulea ai mea tāua. Mo sifi tāua, femalagaaiga, togafitiga faʻafomaʻi, suega, malaga vaalele, aso faʻatapulaʻa faʻaletulafono, mea e tāua tau tupe ma isi galuega tāua, ia faʻaaogā nisi faʻamanatu ma siaki tutoʻatasi.

E lē tatau ona faʻaaogā le polokalame o se vaega o faiga e ono afaina ai le ola, soifua maloloina, saogalemu, meatotino, felauaiga, masini tau alamanuia, masini faʻafomaʻi, faiga o puipuiga po o tali atu i faʻalavelave faafuaseʻi ona o se mea sese po o se tuai.

4. Faʻailo nofoaga ma le nofoaga

O faʻailo nofoaga e faʻalagolago i GPS, Wi-Fi, upega feaveaʻi, le saʻo o le nofoaga, faʻaaogāina i totonu o fale, mafiafia o fale, tulaga o le tau, nofoaga i tua, tapulaa a Android, faʻatulagaga o le maa, radius o le geofence, lelei o faʻafanua ma le maua o auaunaga i fafo.

E ono tuai ona amata se faʻailo nofoaga, lē amata, amata i se auala lē saʻo po o faʻatapulaʻaina e le faiga. E lē fuafuaina faʻailo nofoaga mo le sailia faʻalilolilo o tagata, mataʻituina o tagata faigaluega, galuega o puipuiga, lapataiga o faʻalavelave faafuaseʻi po o galuega e ono afaina ai se tasi ona o se mea sese o le nofoaga.

5. Faʻafanua ma tuatusi

E ono lē atoatoa, tuai, lē saʻo po o lē maua faʻafanua, tuatusi, faʻamaopoopoga ma taunuuga o reverse geocoding. O se tuatusi ua fuafuaina mai faʻamaopoopoga o se faʻamatalaga fesoasoani ma e lē o se faʻamaoniga saʻo faʻaletulafono o le nofoaga.

6. Leo ma tusitusiga leo

O le iloa o le tautala, e aofia ai tusitusiga leo offline ma online, e ono iai ni mea sese, upu e misia, lē saʻo le iloa o igoa, numera, aso, upu faʻapitoa, gagana ma fuaiupu. E tatau i le tagata faʻaaogā ona siaki tutoʻatasi faʻamaumauga tāua.

E lē tatau ona faʻaaogā puʻeina leo ma tusitusiga leo o le puna e tasi mo faʻamatalaga tāua faʻaletulafono, faʻafomaʻi, tau tupe, HR po o isi faʻamatalaga mataʻutia.

7. Totogi ma fuafuaga

O galuega mo totogi, ponesi, toesega, faʻasalaga, coefficient o sifi ma lipoti o galuega fesoasoani ma faʻamatalaga. E lē o ni fautuaga tau tausitusi, lafoga, tulafono po o HR.

E tatau i le tagata faʻaaogā ona faʻamaonia tutoʻatasi fuafuaga e faʻatusatusa i pepa aloaia a le falefaigaluega, konekarate o galuega, payslips, tulafono o lafoga ma le tulafono talafeagai.

8. Kopi faʻaleoleo ma le leiloa o faʻamatalaga

E lē faʻamaonia e le tagata atiaʻe le faʻasaoina o faʻamatalaga pe afai e leʻi faia e le tagata faʻaaogā se kopi faʻaleoleo, ua leiloa le faila, teu i se nofoaga e lē maua, tape faʻamatalaga o le polokalame, faʻamamā le teuina, sui masini, faʻaaogā se faila ua faʻaleagaina, tape faʻatagaga e manaomia po o faʻasoa le faila i isi vaega.

E ono aofia i kopi faʻaleoleo faʻamatalaga patino. O le tagata faʻaaogā e nafa ma le teuina, lafoina, puipuia ma tapeina.

9. Auaunaga a isi vaega

E ono faʻalagolago nisi galuega i faleoloa o polokalame, GitHub po o isi puna mamao, OpenStreetMap/osmdroid, auaunaga geocoding, auaunaga iloa leo a Android, auaunaga imeli, teuina ao, le operating system ma le gaosi masini.

E lē nafa le tagata atiaʻe ma le maua, saʻo, saogalemu, tulafono, suiga, faʻaletonu, tapulaa po o gaioiga a auaunaga a isi vaega ua filifilia e le tagata faʻaaogā po o tuuina mai e le operating system.

10. Gaioiga a le tagata faʻaaogā

O le tagata faʻaaogā e nafa ma le faʻatulagaina saʻo o le polokalame, faʻasologa, faʻailo, geofences, faʻatagaga, faʻasilasilaga, maa, galuega i tua, kopi faʻaleoleo, taimi o le faiga ma le siakiina o le faʻatuatuaina o galuega i lana lava masini.

11. Faʻatapulaʻaina o noataga

I le tapulaa maualuga e faʻatagaina e le tulafono talafeagai, e lē noatia le tagata atiaʻe mo ni faʻaleagaina lē tuusaʻo, tupe mama ua leiloa, faʻasalaga, taunuuga tau amio pulea i galuega, sifi ua misia, tuai, tupe gau, leiloa o faʻamatalaga, fuafuaga sese, mea sese a le tagata faʻaaogā, faʻaletonu o le masini, tapulaa a Android, gaioiga a gaosi masini, fesootaiga, faleoloa o polokalame po o auaunaga a isi vaega.

E leai se mea i lenei pepa e faʻatapulaʻaina ai aia tatau a le tagata faʻaaogā e lē mafai ona faʻatapulaʻaina i lalo o le tulafono faʻamalosia talafeagai.

12. Pepa e fesootaʻi i ai

O lenei Faʻamatalaga e Faʻatapulaʻa ai Noataga e faʻamamaluina faʻatasi ma Tuʻutuʻuga o le Faʻaaogāina ma le Faiga Faʻavae mo le Puipuiga o Faʻamatalaga Patino a ShiftAlarm. Afai e lē malie le tagata faʻaaogā i nei tapulaa ma lamatiaga, e tatau ona taofia le faʻaaogāina o le polokalame.
