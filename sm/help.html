Taiala mo le tagata faʻaaogā o ShiftAlarm

Version 1. Aso: 2026-06-23.

1. Tatalaina muamua

Tatala le polokalame, filifili le gagana e manaomia, siaki faʻatagaga ma faʻatulaga le faʻatuatuaina o faʻailo. I nisi masini, e ono manaomia foʻi le faʻatagaina o galuega i tua ma aveese tapulaa o le maa.

2. Faʻailo

Fai faʻailo masani, faʻailo e toe fai i aso, faʻailo mo se aso faʻapitoa ma faʻailo e fesootaʻi i sifi. Mo faʻailo tāua, siaki faʻasilasilaga, faʻailo saʻo ma tapulaa o le maa.

3. Faʻailo nofoaga

Fai se faʻailo nofoaga i le faʻafanua e ala i le oomi umi. Mo le faʻatuatuaina lelei, faʻaaogā se radius e 800–1000 m ma faʻataga le nofoaga saʻo ma le nofoaga i tua.

4. Sifi

Fai se faʻasologa o sifi o loo ola. E faʻaaogā e faʻailo e fesootaʻi i sifi ma widget o le kalena le faʻasologa o loo ola ma amanaia suiga o aso.

5. Faʻamatalaga ma faʻamanatu

Faʻaaogā faʻamatalaga tusitusia, lisi, mea tutupu, faʻamanatu, faʻamatalaga leo ma tusitusiga leo. E mafai ona laʻu mai faʻataʻitaʻiga iloa leo offline i faʻatulagaga.

6. Widget

Faʻaopoopo se Today Widget po o Calendar Widget i le home screen o Android. E faʻaali e widget faʻamatalaga lata mai ma faʻafou otometi, ma e iai foʻi se faʻamau e faʻafou ai i le lima.

7. Kopi faʻaleoleo

E mafai i Faʻatulagaga ona teu se kopi faʻaleoleo i le telefoni po o faʻasoa e ala i le Android share sheet.

8. Suʻesuʻega

Afai e lē galue saʻo se galuega, tatala le ogalaau o suʻesuʻega talafeagai, kopi po o auina atu i le tagata atiaʻe faʻatasi ma se faʻamatalaga o le faʻafitauli.
