Kaupapahere Tūmataiti o ShiftAlarm

Putanga 2. Rā: 2026-06-29.

1. Te mātāpono whānui mō te tukatuka raraunga

Ko te aronga matua a ShiftAlarm he tauira tuimotu-tuatahi. Ka waihangatia, ka tukatukahia, ka penapenahia ngā raraunga matua a te kaiwhakamahi ki tōna ake pūrere. Kāore te kaiwhakawhanake e whiwhi aunoa i aua raraunga, ki te kore te kaiwhakamahi e tuku mā tētahi tono tautoko, kaweake, īmēra, karere, rokiroki kapua, toa taupānga, mā tētahi atu tikanga rānei i tīpakohia e ia.

I tēnei putanga, kāore te taupānga e whakamahi i ngā whatunga pānuitanga, i tōna ake pūnaha pūkete, i te whakamana taha-tūmau, i Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Crashlytics, AdMob rānei, ki te kore e whakapuakina māriretia i tētahi putanga kua whakahoutia o tēnei Kaupapahere.

2. Ngā raraunga ka penapenahia pea ki te pūrere

I runga i ngā āhuatanga e whakamahia ana, ka penapena pea te taupānga i ēnei raraunga ki te pūrere:

  • ngā tautuhinga taupānga, te reo, te kaupapa, ngā tautuhinga widget me ngā manakohanga a te kaiwhakamahi;
  • ngā whakaoho auau, ngā whakaoho hono-huringa, te wā, ngā tukurua, ngā oro, te wiri, te whakaroa, te whakapiki oro āta me ngā tautuhinga e hāngai ana;
  • ngā hōtaka huringa mahi, ngā tauira huringa, ngā panoni maramataka, ngā rā whakatā me ngā tohu a te kaiwhakamahi;
  • ngā whakaoho wāhi: ngā taunga, te pūtoro, te momo takahanga urunga/putanga, te wāhitau, te ingoa wāhi, ngā wāhi kua tiakina, te hōtaka me te tūnga whakakā;
  • ngā tuhipoka, ngā takahanga, ngā whakamaumahara, ngā rārangi taki, ngā poraka kuputuhi me ngā rā e hāngai ana;
  • ngā tuhipoka oro, ngā hopukanga reo me ngā tuhinga reo;
  • te matawā, te karaka tū, ngā karaka o te ao me te hītori o ētahi mahi mēnā he wāhanga taua hītori nō te āhuatanga;
  • ngā tautuhinga tātaitanga utu mahi, ngā tāpiritanga, ngā tangohanga, ngā whaina, ngā pūrongo me ngā tātaitanga e hāngai ana;
  • ngā rangitaki tātaritanga, ngā takahanga hangarau, te tūnga whakaaetanga, ngā hapa me ngā tūnga āhuatanga;
  • ngā tārua mēnā ka waihangatia ā-ringa e te kaiwhakamahi.

3. Ngā raraunga ka tukuna pea e te kaiwhakamahi ki te kaiwhakawhanake

Ki te whakapā atu te kaiwhakamahi ki te tautoko i support@insdeco.ru, ka whiwhi pea te kaiwhakawhanake i te wāhitau īmēra o te kaiwhakamahi, te ingoa, te waitohu īmēra rānei, te kuputuhi karere, ngā āpitihanga, ngā whakaahua mata, ngā kōnae tārua, ngā pūrongo tātaritanga, te tauira pūrere, te putanga Android, te putanga taupānga, te wā o te takahanga me te whakamārama o te take.

Ka tukuna aua raraunga mā te mahi a te kaiwhakamahi anake. Me arotake te kaiwhakamahi i te īmēra me ngā āpitihanga i mua i te tuku.

4. Ngā kaupapa o te tukatuka

Ka whakamahia ngā raraunga hei whakahaere i ngā āhuatanga o te taupānga, hei tiaki i ngā tautuhinga a te kaiwhakamahi, hei tuku whakaoho me ngā whakamaumahara, hei whakahaere i ngā whakaoho wāhi, hei whakaatu tuhipoka me ngā widget, hei waihanga tārua, hei kimi hapa, hei tukatuka tono tautoko, hei whakapai i te pūmautanga o te taupānga, hei whakatutuki i ngā herenga ā-ture, hei tiaki hoki i ngā motika o te kaiwhakawhanake me te kaiwhakamahi.

I runga i te ture e hāngai ana, ko ngā tūāpapa ā-ture mō te tukatuka pea ko te whakatutuki i te whakaaetanga kaiwhakamahi, te mahi a te kaiwhakamahi, te whakaaetanga mō ngā whakaaetanga motuhake, te pānga tika mō te tautoko me te haumaru, me te whakatutuki i ngā herenga ā-ture.

5. Ngā whakaaetanga pūrere

Ka tono noa te taupānga i ngā whakaaetanga e hiahiatia ana mō ngā āhuatanga e hāngai ana:

  • ngā whakamōhiotanga — mō ngā whakaoho, ngā matawā, ngā whakamaumahara me ngā whakamōhiotanga ratonga;
  • ngā whakaoho pū me ngā whakaaetanga whakaoho — kia whakakā i te wā i tīpakohia e te kaiwhakamahi;
  • he tauwāhi i te wā e whakamahia ana te taupānga — mō te tīpako wāhi, ngā mahere me te whakarite whakaoho wāhi;
  • te tauwāhi papamuri — mō ngā whakaoho wāhi i te wā kāore te taupānga e tuwhera ana;
  • te hopuoro — mō ngā tuhipoka oro, te tāuru reo me te mōhiotanga reo;
  • te ipurangi — mō te tikiake mōkihi reo, tuhinga ā-ture, āwhina, tohutohu, tauira tuhi-reo tuimotu, mahere me ngā āhuatanga whatunga;
  • te urunga kōnae mā te kaiwhiri kōnae pūnaha — mō te kaweake me te kawemai tārua;
  • ngā tautuhinga pūhiko me te mahi papamuri — hei whakapai i te pono o ngā whakaoho, ngā whakamōhiotanga me ngā whakaoho wāhi.

Ka taea e te kaiwhakamahi te tango whakaaetanga i ngā tautuhinga Android. I muri i te tangohanga, ka kore pea ētahi āhuatanga e wātea, ka mahi rānei me ngā here.

6. Te tauwāhi me ngā mahere

Ka whakamahia ngā raraunga tauwāhi hei tīpako i tētahi pūwāhi i te mahere, hei tiaki whakaoho wāhi, hei kite i te urunga atu, te putanga atu rānei i tētahi rohe kua tautuhia, hei whakaatu wāhitau, hei waihanga hoki i ngā wāhi kua tiakina. Ka penapenahia ngā taunga me ngā tautuhinga whakaoho wāhi ki te pūrere, ki te kore te kaiwhakamahi e kaweake, e tuku rānei i aua raraunga.

Ina whakamahi te kaiwhakamahi i ngā mahere, i te rapu wāhitau rānei, ka whakapā pea te taupānga ki ngā wāhanga mahere me te whakawaehere wāhi, tae atu ki osmdroid/OpenStreetMap, te whakawaehere wāhi whakamuri a Nominatim, te Android Geocoder pūnaha, tētahi atu ratonga rānei e wātea ana i te pūrere. Ka whiwhi pea aua ratonga i ngā raraunga tono hangarau, te wāhitau IP, ngā taunga, ētahi atu raraunga rānei e hiahiatia ana hei whakaatu i te mahere, hei whakatau wāhitau rānei.

7. Te oro, te tuhi-reo tuimotu me te tuhi-reo tuihono

Ka waihangatia ngā tuhipoka oro me ngā tuhinga reo mā te mahi a te kaiwhakamahi, ā, ka whai pea i ngā mōhiohio whaiaro, tairongo rānei mēnā ka hopukina e te kaiwhakamahi. Ka penapenahia ki te pūrere, ki te kore te kaiwhakamahi e kaweake, e tuku rānei.

Ka whakamahi pea te tuhi-reo tuimotu i ngā tauira mōhiotanga reo kua tikiakehia ki te pūrere. Ina tikiakehia aua tauira, ka kite pea te tūmau mamao i ngā raraunga hononga hangarau pērā i te wāhitau IP me ngā mōhiohio tono.

Ka whakamahi pea te mōhiotanga reo tuihono i te ratonga mōhiotanga reo Android pūnaha i tīpakohia e te pūrere, te kaiwhakanao, te kaiwhakamahi, te pūnaha whakahaere rānei. I taua wā, ka tukuna pea ngā raraunga oro me ngā mōhiohio hangarau ki te ratonga mōhiotanga reo kua tīpakohia, ka tukatukahia hoki i raro i āna ake ture. Kāore te kaiwhakawhanake o ShiftAlarm e whakahaere i ngā kaupapahere o taua ratonga pūnaha.

8. Ngā tikiake whatunga me ngā puna mamao

Ka whakamahi pea te taupānga i te ipurangi ki te tikiake mōkihi reo, kōnae manipeheti, tuhinga ā-ture, rauemi āwhina, tohutohu me ngā tauira tuhi-reo tuimotu, tae atu mai i GitHub, i tētahi atu puna mamao kua tautuhia ki roto i te taupānga rānei. I aua tono, ka whiwhi pea te tūmau mamao i te wāhitau IP, te rā me te wā o te tono, te ara kōnae, ngā mōhiohio hononga me ētahi atu raraunga hangarau paerewa.

Ka taea te manatoko i ngā kōnae kua whakatinanahia he arowhai tapatahi ki roto i te taupānga mā SHA-256, mā tētahi atu tapeke arowhai rānei. Ka whakaiti tēnei i te tūponotanga o te pirau kōnae, engari kāore e pūtāhui i te wāteatanga o te puna mamao.

Mā te toa taupānga tonu e tukatuka te pūkete a te kaiwhakamahi, ngā utu, ngā whakahoki moni, te hītori hoko, te tāutanga me ngā whakahou i raro i āna ake ture me tōna kaupapahere tūmataiti.

Kāore te kaiwhakawhanake e whiwhi i ngā kāri pēke, ngā kupuhipa utu, ngā taipitopito utu katoa rānei ina utua mā te toa taupānga.
