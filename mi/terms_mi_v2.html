Ngā Tikanga Whakamahi o ShiftAlarm

Putanga 2. Rā: 2026-06-29.

1. Ngā tikanga whānui

Ko ēnei Tikanga Whakamahi e whakahaere ana i te whakamahinga o te taupānga pūkoro ShiftAlarm, ōna āhuatanga, ngā raraunga paetata, ngā rauemi āwhina, ngā tuhinga ā-ture me ngā whakahou ka tukuna mā ngā toa taupānga, mā ngā puna taupānga mamao rānei.

Mā te tāuta, te whakatuwhera, te whakamahi rānei i a ShiftAlarm, ka whakaū te kaiwhakamahi kua pānui, kua whakaae hoki ia ki ēnei Tikanga, ki te Kaupapahere Tūmataiti me te Whakakape Kawenga. Ki te kore te kaiwhakamahi e whakaae ki ēnei tuhinga, me whakamutu te whakamahi i te taupānga, me tango hoki i te pūrere.

2. Te kaupapa o te taupānga

Kua hangaia a ShiftAlarm hei whakarite whaiaro i ngā hōtaka huringa mahi, ngā whakaoho auau, ngā whakaoho hono-huringa, ngā whakaoho wāhi, ngā tuhipoka, ngā takahanga, ngā whakamaumahara, ngā tuhipoka oro, ngā tuhinga reo, te matawā, te karaka tū, ngā karaka o te ao, ngā widget, ngā tārua me ngā tātaitanga utu mahi āwhina.

He taputapu āwhina te taupānga. Ehara tēnei i te pūnaha haumaru ahumahi, i te ratonga hauora, ohotata, haumaru, kaute, tāke, utu kaimahi, HR, ture rānei.

3. Te pakeke me te āheinga ā-ture

Tērā pea he whakatauranga pakeke 0+ tō te taupānga i tētahi toa taupānga, ki te kore e kīia he rerekē i te whārangi o te toa. Ko te tikanga o taua whakatauranga kāore ngā ihirangi o te taupānga e herea nuitia e te pakeke, engari kāore taua whakatauranga e whakakapi i ngā ture e pā ana ki te āheinga ā-ture, te whakaaetanga a te mātua, te kaitiaki ā-ture rānei.

Ki te kore e taea e te kaiwhakamahi te whakauru takitahi ki ngā whakaaetanga whai mana ā-ture, te tuku rānei i ngā whakaaetanga e hiahiatia ana i raro i ngā ture o tōna whenua, me whakamahi te taupānga me te whai wāhi mai o tētahi mātua, kaitiaki, māngai ā-ture kē atu rānei.

4. Te urunga, te pūrere me ngā whakaaetanga

Ka hiahiatia pea e ētahi āhuatanga tētahi putanga Android hototahi, te wā pūnaha tika, ngā whakaaetanga mō ngā whakamōhiotanga, ngā whakaoho pū, te tauwāhi, te tauwāhi papamuri, te hopuoro, te ipurangi, te urunga kōnae mā te kaiwhiri kōnae pūnaha, me ngā tautuhinga pūhiko me te mahi papamuri.

Kei te kaiwhakamahi te kawenga ki te tuku, te tango me te tirotiro i ngā whakaaetanga i ngā tautuhinga Android, ā, e mārama ana ia mēnā ka monokia ngā whakaaetanga, ka iti pea te mahi a ētahi āhuatanga, ka tōmuri, kāore rānei e mahi.

5. Te hoko me ngā āhuatanga utu

Ka tohaina a ShiftAlarm hei taupānga ka hokona kotahi mō te pūkete o te kaiwhakamahi i te toa taupānga i oti ai te hoko, pērā i RuStore, Google Play rānei, mēnā e wātea ana te taupānga i taua tūāpapa.

Ko te utu, te whakaū hoko, te whakahoki moni, te whakahoki hoko, te tiritiri ā-whānau, te whakawhiti ki tētahi atu pūrere me ērā atu take pūkete ka whakahaerehia i raro i ngā ture o te toa taupānga e hāngai ana. Kāore te kaiwhakawhanake e whiwhi, e tukatuka rānei i ngā kāri pēke, ngā taipitopito utu, ngā kupuhipa pūkete rānei ina utua mā te toa taupānga.

Ki te tāpirihia ā muri ake nei he ohaurunga, he hoko tāpiri, he āhuatanga utu kē atu rānei, ka whakahoutia ngā tikanga i mua i te whakamahinga o aua āhuatanga, i runga rānei i te tikanga e hiahiatia ana e te toa taupānga e hāngai ana.

6. Ngā raraunga paetata me ngā ihirangi a te kaiwhakamahi

Mā te kaiwhakamahi tonu e waihanga, e whakarerekē, e muku hoki ngā raraunga i roto i te taupānga: ngā hōtaka huringa mahi, ngā whakaoho, ngā pūwāhi wāhi, ngā taunga, ngā wāhi kua tiakina, ngā tuhipoka, ngā hopukanga oro, ngā tuhinga reo, ngā tātaitanga utu mahi, ngā tātaritanga, ngā tautuhinga me ngā tārua.

Kei te kaiwhakamahi te kawenga mō te tika ā-ture, te tika o te ihirangi, te haumarutanga me te hāngaitanga o aua raraunga, me te whakaute i ngā motika o te hunga tuatoru ina waihanga tuhipoka, hopukanga oro, tuhinga reo me ngā tārua.

Mā te taunoa ka penapenahia ngā raraunga taupānga ki te pūrere. Ki te kaweake, tuku, tārua, penapena rānei te kaiwhakamahi i ngā raraunga mā tētahi atu taupānga, ratonga kapua, īmēra, karere, kaiwhakahaere kōnae, rokiroki waho rānei, ka whakawhirinaki te tukatuka o muri mai ki te ratonga, ki te wāhi rokiroki rānei i tīpakohia e te kaiwhakamahi.

Ka whakaae te kaiwhakamahi kia whakamahia te taupānga i runga i te ture anake, mō te whakarite whaiaro i te wā, ngā huringa mahi, ngā whakamaumahara, ngā whakaoho, ngā whakaoho wāhi, ngā tuhipoka me ngā āhuatanga e hāngai ana.
