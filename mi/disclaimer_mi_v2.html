Whakakape Kawenga a ShiftAlarm

Putanga 2. Rā: 2026-06-29.

He taupānga āwhina a ShiftAlarm mō te whakarite whaiaro i ngā huringa mahi, ngā whakaoho, ngā whakaoho wāhi, ngā tuhipoka, ngā whakamaumahara, te matawā, te karaka tū, ngā widget, ngā tārua me ngā āhuatanga e hāngai ana. Ehara te taupānga i te pūnaha haumaru whai-pūtāhui, matohi ohotata, aroturuki hauora, HR, kaute, utu kaimahi rānei.

1. Ka tukuna te taupānga “kei tōna āhua o nāianei”

Ka tukuna te taupānga “kei tōna āhua o nāianei”, “e wātea ana” hoki. Ka whai te kaiwhakawhanake i ngā mahi tōtika hei pupuri i te pūmautanga o te taupānga, engari kāore e pūtāhui ka mahi ngā āhuatanga katoa me te kore hapa, tōmuritanga, takahanga ngaro, hototahi-kore, ngaronga raraunga, here rānei i ia pūrere, putanga Android, pūmanawa kaiwhakanao me ngā āhuatanga whakamahinga katoa.

2. Kāore he pūtāhui tino mō te tuku

Ka whakawhirinaki ngā whakaoho, ngā whakaoho hono-huringa, ngā whakaoho wāhi, ngā matawā, ngā whakamaumahara, ngā whakamōhiotanga me ngā widget ki Android, te kaiwhakanao pūrere, ngā whakaaetanga, te wā pūnaha tika, te aratau whakaora pūhiko, ngā here mahi papamuri, te tūnga pūhiko, te aratau Do Not Disturb, ngā tautuhinga whakamōhiotanga, ngā tīmatanga-anō, ngā whakahou OS, te kati ā-ringa i te taupānga, ngā hapa pūnaha me ngā mahi a te kaiwhakamahi.

Ka tōmuri pea te tuku, ka ngaro, ka puta i tētahi wā rerekē i te mea i whakaarotia, ka puta me te kore oro, whakamōhiotanga rānei, ka herea rānei e te pūnaha.

3. Ngā takahanga hira me te whakamahinga tino hira

Kaua e whakamahi i a ShiftAlarm hei tikanga whakahaere kotahi mō ngā takahanga tino hira. Mō ngā huringa mahi hira, ngā haerenga, ngā tukanga hauora, ngā whakamātautau, ngā rerenga, ngā rā kati ā-ture, ngā takahanga whai tikanga pūtea me ētahi atu mahi hira, whakamahia ētahi atu whakamaumahara me ngā arowhai motuhake.

Kaua te taupānga e whakamahia hei wāhanga o ngā pūnaha e taea ai e tētahi hapa, tōmuritanga rānei te whara i te oranga, te hauora, te haumaru, te rawa, te waka, ngā taputapu ahumahi, ngā pūrere hauora, ngā pūnaha haumaru, te urupare ohotata rānei.

4. Ngā whakaoho wāhi me te tauwāhi

Ka whakawhirinaki ngā whakaoho wāhi ki GPS, Wi-Fi, ngā whatunga pūkoro, te tika o te tauwāhi, te whakamahinga i roto whare, te kiato o ngā whare, ngā āhuatanga huarere, te tauwāhi papamuri, ngā here pūnaha Android, ngā tautuhinga pūhiko, te pūtoro geofence, te kounga mahere me te wāteatanga o ngā ratonga waho.

Ka whakakā tōmuri pea tētahi whakaoho wāhi, kāore pea e whakakā, ka whakakā hē, ka herea rānei e te pūnaha. Kāore ngā whakaoho wāhi i hangaia mō te whai huna i ngā tāngata, te aroturuki kaimahi, ngā mahi haumaru, ngā matohi ohotata, ngā mahi rānei e taea ai e tētahi hapa tauwāhi te whakaputa whara.

5. Ngā mahere me ngā wāhitau

Ka hē, ka tawhito, ka kore e oti, ka kore rānei e wātea ngā mahere, ngā wāhitau, ngā taunga me ngā hua whakawaehere wāhi whakamuri. He mōhiohio āwhina noa te wāhitau ka whakatauria mai i ngā taunga, ehara i te whakaū ā-ture tino tika o te tauwāhi.

6. Te oro me ngā tuhinga reo

Ka whai hapa pea te mōhiotanga reo, tae atu ki te tuhi-reo tuimotu me te tuihono, pērā i te mahue o ngā kupu, te mōhio hē ki ngā ingoa, ngā tau, ngā rā, ngā kupu hangarau, ngā reo me ngā rerenga. Me tirotiro takitahi te kaiwhakamahi i ngā rekoata hira.

Kaua ngā hopukanga oro me ngā tuhinga reo e whakamahia hei puna kotahi mō ngā mōhiohio whai tikanga ā-ture, hauora, pūtea, HR, mōhiohio tino hira kē atu rānei.

7. Te utu mahi me ngā tātaitanga

He āhuatanga āwhina, whakamōhio hoki ngā mahi utu, tāpiritanga, tangohanga, whaina, whakarea huringa me ngā pūrongo. Ehara ēnei i te tohutohu kaute, tāke, ture, HR rānei.

Me manatoko takitahi te kaiwhakamahi i ngā tātaitanga ki ngā tuhinga whaimana a te kaituku mahi, ngā kirimana mahi, ngā pepa utu, ngā ture tāke me te ture e hāngai ana.

8. Ngā tārua me te ngaronga raraunga

Kāore te kaiwhakawhanake e pūtāhui i te tiakitanga o ngā raraunga mēnā kāore te kaiwhakamahi i waihanga tārua, kua ngaro te kōnae, kua penapena ki tētahi wāhi kāore e wātea, kua muku raraunga taupānga, kua ūkui rokiroki, kua huri pūrere, kua whakamahi kōnae pakaru, kua monokia ngā whakaaetanga e hiahiatia ana, kua tiri rānei i te kōnae ki te hunga tuatoru.

Kei roto pea i ngā tārua ngā mōhiohio whaiaro. Kei te kaiwhakamahi te kawenga mō te penapena, te whakawhiti, te tiaki me te muku.

9. Ngā ratonga a te hunga tuatoru

Ka whakawhirinaki pea ētahi āhuatanga ki ngā toa taupānga, GitHub, ētahi atu puna mamao rānei, OpenStreetMap/osmdroid, ngā ratonga whakawaehere wāhi, te ratonga mōhiotanga reo Android pūnaha, ngā ratonga īmēra, te rokiroki kapua, te pūnaha whakahaere me te kaiwhakanao pūrere.

Kāore te kaiwhakawhanake e kawenga mō te wāteatanga, te tika, te haumarutanga, ngā ture, ngā whakarerekētanga, ngā rahunga, ngā here, ngā mahi rānei a ngā ratonga a te hunga tuatoru i tīpakohia e te kaiwhakamahi, i tukuna rānei e te pūnaha whakahaere.

10. Ngā mahi a te kaiwhakamahi

Kei te kaiwhakamahi te kawenga mō te whakarite tika i te taupānga, ngā hōtaka, ngā whakaoho, ngā geofence, ngā whakaaetanga, ngā whakamōhiotanga, te pūhiko, te mahi papamuri, ngā tārua, te wā pūnaha, me te whakamātautau i te pono o ngā āhuatanga i tōna ake pūrere.

11. Te herenga o te kawenga

Ki te whānuitanga mōrahi e whakaaetia ana e te ture e hāngai ana, kāore te kaiwhakawhanake e kawenga mō ngā wharanga autaki, ngā hua kua ngaro, ngā whaina, ngā hua whiu mahi, ngā huringa mahi kua mahue, te tōmuri, ngā ngaronga pūtea, te ngaronga raraunga, ngā tātaitanga hē, ngā hapa a te kaiwhakamahi, ngā rahunga pūrere, ngā here Android, ngā mahi a ngā kaiwhakanao pūrere, ngā whatunga whakawhitiwhiti, ngā toa taupānga, ngā ratonga a te hunga tuatoru rānei.

Kāore tētahi mea i roto i tēnei tuhinga e whakaiti i ngā motika o te kaiwhakamahi kāore e taea te whakaiti i raro i te ture whakahau e hāngai ana.

12. Ngā tuhinga e hāngai ana

Ka mana tēnei Whakakape Kawenga me ngā Tikanga Whakamahi me te Kaupapahere Tūmataiti o ShiftAlarm. Ki te kore te kaiwhakamahi e whakaae ki ēnei here me ngā tūraru, me whakamutu te whakamahi i te taupānga.
