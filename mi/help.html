Aratohu kaiwhakamahi ShiftAlarm

Putanga 1. Rā: 2026-06-23.

1. Te whakatuwheratanga tuatahi

Whakatuwheratia te taupānga, tīpakohia te reo e hiahiatia ana, tirohia ngā whakaaetanga, ā, whirihorahia te pono o ngā whakaoho. I ētahi pūrere, me whakaae hoki pea te mahi papamuri, me tango hoki ngā here pūhiko.

2. Ngā whakaoho

Waihangatia ngā whakaoho auau, ngā whakaoho tukurua ā-rā, ngā whakaoho rā motuhake me ngā whakaoho hono-huringa. Mō ngā whakaoho hira, tirohia ngā whakamōhiotanga, ngā whakaoho pū me ngā here pūhiko.

3. Ngā whakaoho wāhi

Waihangatia tētahi whakaoho wāhi i te mahere mā te pēhi roa. Kia pono ake, whakamahia tētahi pūtoro 800–1000 m, ā, whakaaetia te tauwāhi pū me te tauwāhi papamuri.

4. Ngā huringa mahi

Waihangatia tētahi hōtaka huringa mahi hohe. Ka whakamahi ngā whakaoho hono-huringa me ngā widget maramataka i te hōtaka hohe, ā, ka whai whakaaro ki ngā panoni rā.

5. Ngā tuhipoka me ngā whakamaumahara

Whakamahia ngā tuhipoka kuputuhi, ngā rārangi, ngā takahanga, ngā whakamaumahara, ngā tuhipoka oro me ngā tuhinga reo. Ka taea te tikiake i ngā tauira reo tuimotu i ngā tautuhinga.

6. Ngā widget

Tāpirihia he Widget o Tēnei Rā, he Widget Maramataka rānei ki te mata kāinga Android. Ka whakaatu ngā widget i ngā mōhiohio tata, ka whakahou aunoa hoki, ā, he pātene whakahou ā-ringa anō tō aua widget.

7. Ngā tārua

Ka āhei ngā Tautuhinga ki te tiaki tārua ki te waea, ki te tiri rānei mā te whārangi tiritiri Android.

8. Ngā tātaritanga

Ki te kore tētahi āhuatanga e mahi tika, whakatuwheratia te rangitaki tātaritanga e hāngai ana, ka tārua, ka tuku rānei ki te kaiwhakawhanake me tētahi whakamārama o te raru.
