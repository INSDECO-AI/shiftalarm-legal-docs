Itangazo ryo kutaryozwa rya ShiftAlarm

Verisiyo 2. Itariki: 2026-06-29.

ShiftAlarm ni porogaramu y'ubufasha igenewe gutegura ku giti cye ibihe by'akazi, impuruza, impuruza z'ahantu, inyandiko, ibyibutsa, igipima gihe, isaha-mpagarika, widget, kopi z'ububiko n'imikorere bifitanye isano. Porogaramu si sisitemu yizewe burundu y'umutekano, imenyesha ry'ubutabazi, gukurikirana ubuzima, imicungire y'abakozi, ibaruramari cyangwa kwishyura imishahara.

1. Porogaramu itangwa “uko iri”

Porogaramu itangwa “uko iri” kandi “uko iboneka”. Uwakoze porogaramu afata ingamba zishyize mu gaciro zo kuyigumisha ikora neza, ariko ntiyizeza ko imikorere yose izakora ku gikoresho cyose, kuri verisiyo yose ya Android, kuri firmware y'uruganda urwo ari rwo rwose no mu buryo bwose bwo kuyikoresha, nta makosa, gutinda, ibikorwa bitabaye, kudahuza, gutakaza amakuru cyangwa imipaka.

2. Nta cyizere cyo gutangwa burundu

Impuruza, impuruza zishingiye ku gihe cy'akazi, impuruza z'ahantu, ibipima gihe, ibyibutsa, imenyesha na widget bishingira kuri Android, uruganda rw'igikoresho, uburenganzira, igihe cya sisitemu gishyizweho neza, uburyo bwo kuzigama batiri, imipaka yo gukora inyuma, imiterere ya batiri, uburyo bwa Do Not Disturb, igenamiterere ry'imenyesha, kongera gutangiza igikoresho, amavugurura ya sisitemu y'imikorere, gufunga porogaramu n'intoki, amakosa ya sisitemu n'ibikorwa by'umukoresha.

Impuruza, ibyibutsa cyangwa imenyesha bishobora gutinda, kutabaho, gukora ku gihe gitandukanye n'icyari cyitezwe, gukora nta jwi cyangwa imenyesha, cyangwa kugabanywa na sisitemu.

3. Ibikorwa by'ingenzi n'ikoreshwa rikomeye

Ntukoreshe ShiftAlarm nk'uburyo bwonyine bwo kugenzura ibikorwa bikomeye. Ku bihe by'akazi by'ingenzi, ingendo, ibikorwa by'ubuvuzi, ibizamini, indege, igihe ntarengwa mu mategeko, ibikorwa bifite agaciro kanini k'amafaranga n'indi mirimo y'ingenzi, koresha ubundi buryo bwigenga bwo kwibutsa no kugenzura.

Porogaramu ntigomba gukoreshwa nk'igice cya sisitemu aho ikosa cyangwa gutinda bishobora guteza ibyago ku buzima, ubuzima bw'umubiri, umutekano, umutungo, ubwikorezi, ibikoresho by'inganda, ibikoresho by'ubuvuzi, sisitemu z'umutekano cyangwa ubutabazi bwihutirwa.

4. Impuruza z'ahantu n'aho igikoresho giherereye

Impuruza z'ahantu zishingira kuri GPS, Wi-Fi, imiyoboro ya telefoni, ukuri kw'aho igikoresho giherereye, gukoresha mu nzu, ubucucike bw'inyubako, ikirere, uburenganzira bw'aho igikoresho giherereye porogaramu ikorera inyuma, imipaka ya sisitemu ya Android, igenamiterere rya batiri, radiyusi ya geofence, ubwiza bw'ikarita no kuboneka kwa serivisi zo hanze.

Impuruza y'ahantu ishobora gukora itinze, kudakora, gukora mu buryo butari bwo cyangwa kugabanywa na sisitemu. Impuruza z'ahantu ntizigenewe gukurikirana abantu mu ibanga, kugenzura abakozi, imirimo y'umutekano, imenyesha ry'ubutabazi cyangwa imirimo aho ikosa ry'ahantu rishobora guteza ibyago.

5. Amakarita na aderesi

Amakarita, aderesi, ibipimo by'ahantu n'ibisubizo bya reverse geocoding bishobora kuba bituzuye, bishaje, bitari byo cyangwa bitaboneka. Aderesi yabonetse uhereye ku bipimo by'ahantu ni amakuru y'ubufasha kandi si icyemezo cy'aho hantu cyuzuye mu mategeko.

6. Amajwi n'inyandiko zavanywe mu majwi

Kumenya imvugo, harimo guhindura imvugo inyandiko nta murandasi no kuri interineti, bishobora kugira amakosa, ibibura, cyangwa kumenya nabi amazina, imibare, amatariki, amagambo, indimi n'interuro. Umukoresha agomba kugenzura ku giti cye ibyanditswe by'ingenzi.

Amajwi yafashwe n'inyandiko zavanywe mu majwi ntibigomba gukoreshwa nk'isoko ryonyine ry'amakuru afite agaciro mu mategeko, mu buvuzi, mu mafaranga, mu micungire y'abakozi cyangwa andi makuru akomeye.

7. Umushahara n'ibarwa

Imikorere y'umushahara, agahimbazamusyi, ibikurwaho, ibihano, igipimo cy'igihe cy'akazi na raporo ni iy'ubufasha n'amakuru gusa. Si inama y'ibaruramari, imisoro, amategeko cyangwa imicungire y'abakozi.

Umukoresha agomba kugenzura ibarwa ku giti cye akoresheje inyandiko zemewe z'umukoresha, amasezerano y'akazi, impapuro z'umushahara, amategeko y'imisoro n'amategeko akurikizwa.

8. Kopi z'ububiko no gutakaza amakuru

Uwakoze porogaramu ntiyizeza ko amakuru azarindwa iyo umukoresha atakoze kopi y'ububiko, yatakaje dosiye, yayibitse ahataboneka, yasibye amakuru ya porogaramu, yasukuye ububiko, yahinduye igikoresho, yakoresheje dosiye yangiritse, yazimije uburenganzira bukenewe cyangwa yasangiye dosiye n'abandi.

Kopi z'ububiko zishobora kuba zirimo amakuru bwite. Umukoresha ni we ushinzwe kuzibika, kuzohereza, kuzirinda no kuzisiba.

9. Serivisi z'abandi

Imikorere imwe ishobora gushingira ku maduka ya porogaramu, GitHub cyangwa andi masoko ya kure, OpenStreetMap/osmdroid, serivisi zo kumenya aderesi, serivisi ya Android yo kumenya imvugo, serivisi za imeyili, ububiko bwo mu gicu, sisitemu y'imikorere n'uruganda rw'igikoresho.

Uwakoze porogaramu ntaryozwa kuboneka, ukuri, umutekano, amategeko, impinduka, kunanirwa, imipaka cyangwa ibikorwa bya serivisi z'abandi zatoranyijwe n'umukoresha cyangwa zatanzwe na sisitemu y'imikorere.

10. Ibikorwa by'umukoresha

Umukoresha ni we ushinzwe gushyiraho neza porogaramu, gahunda, impuruza, geofence, uburenganzira, imenyesha, batiri, gukora inyuma, kopi z'ububiko, igihe cya sisitemu no kugenzura ukwizerwa kw'imikorere ku gikoresho cye.

11. Imipaka y'uburyozwe

Ku rugero runini rwemewe n'amategeko akurikizwa, uwakoze porogaramu ntaryozwa ibyangiritse bitaziguye, inyungu zatakaye, ibihano, ingaruka z'imyitwarire ku kazi, ibihe by'akazi byatakaye, gukererwa, igihombo cy'amafaranga, gutakaza amakuru, ibarwa ritari ryo, amakosa y'umukoresha, ibibazo by'igikoresho, imipaka ya Android, ibikorwa by'abakora ibikoresho, imiyoboro y'itumanaho, amaduka ya porogaramu cyangwa serivisi z'abandi.

Nta kintu kiri muri iyi nyandiko kigabanya uburenganzira bw'umukoresha budashobora kugabanywa n'amategeko ategetswe akurikizwa.

12. Inyandiko bifitanye isano

Iri Tangazo ryo kutaryozwa rikurikizwa hamwe n'Amasezerano y'imikoreshereze ya ShiftAlarm na Politiki y'ibanga. Iyo umukoresha atemera iyi mipaka n'ibi byago, agomba guhagarika gukoresha porogaramu.
