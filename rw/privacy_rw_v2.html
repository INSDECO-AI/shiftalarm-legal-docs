Politiki y'ibanga ya ShiftAlarm

Verisiyo 2. Itariki: 2026-06-29.

1. Ihame rusange ryo gutunganya amakuru

ShiftAlarm ahanini ikoresha uburyo bwo kubanza gukora nta murandasi. Amakuru y'ingenzi y'umukoresha akorerwa, agatunganyirizwa kandi akabikwa ku gikoresho cye. Uwakoze porogaramu ntayakira byikora keretse umukoresha ayohereje mu busabe bw'ubufasha, mu kuyohereza hanze, kuri imeyili, mu butumwa, mu bubiko bwo mu gicu, mu iduka rya porogaramu cyangwa mu bundi buryo yihitiyemo.

Kuri iyi verisiyo, porogaramu ntikoresha imiyoboro y'iyamamaza, sisitemu yayo ya konti, kwinjira binyuze kuri seriveri, Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Crashlytics cyangwa AdMob, keretse ibyo bivuzwe mu buryo bweruye muri verisiyo yavuguruwe y'iyi Politiki.

2. Amakuru ashobora kubikwa ku gikoresho

Bitewe n'imikorere ikoreshwa, porogaramu ishobora kubika kuri igikoresho amakuru akurikira:

  • igenamiterere rya porogaramu, ururimi, insanganyamatsiko, igenamiterere rya widget n'amahitamo y'umukoresha;
  • impuruza zisanzwe, impuruza zishingiye ku gihe cy'akazi, igihe, gusubiramo, amajwi, kunyeganyega, gusubika, kuzamura ijwi buhoro n'igenamiterere bifitanye isano;
  • gahunda z'akazi, inyandikorugero z'ibihe by'akazi, impinduka kuri kalendari, iminsi y'ikiruhuko n'ibimenyetso by'umukoresha;
  • impuruza z'ahantu: ibipimo by'ahantu, radiyusi, ubwoko bw'igikorwa cyo kwinjira cyangwa gusohoka, aderesi, izina ry'ahantu, ahantu habitswe, gahunda n'imiterere y'uko impuruza ikora;
  • inyandiko, ibikorwa, ibyibutsa, intonde zo kugenzura, ibice by'inyandiko n'amatariki bifitanye isano;
  • inyandiko z'amajwi, amajwi yafashwe n'inyandiko zavanywe mu majwi;
  • igipima gihe, isaha-mpagarika, amasaha y'isi n'amateka y'ibikorwa bimwe iyo ayo mateka ari igice cy'iyo mikorere;
  • igenamiterere ryo kubara umushahara, agahimbazamusyi, ibikurwaho, ibihano, raporo n'ibindi bibarwa bifitanye isano;
  • inyandiko z'isuzuma, ibikorwa bya tekiniki, imiterere y'uburenganzira, amakosa n'imiterere y'imikorere;
  • kopi z'ububiko iyo umukoresha azikoreye ubwe.

3. Amakuru umukoresha ashobora kohereza ku wakoze porogaramu

Iyo umukoresha yandikiye ubufasha kuri support@insdeco.ru, uwakoze porogaramu ashobora kwakira aderesi ya imeyili y'umukoresha, izina cyangwa umukono wa imeyili, ubutumwa, imigereka, amashusho ya ecran, dosiye za kopi z'ububiko, raporo z'isuzuma, ubwoko bw'igikoresho, verisiyo ya Android, verisiyo ya porogaramu, igihe igikorwa cyabereye n'ibisobanuro by'ikibazo.

Ayo makuru yoherezwa gusa n'igikorwa cy'umukoresha. Umukoresha agomba kubanza kugenzura imeyili n'imigereka mbere yo kubyohereza.

4. Intego zo gutunganya amakuru

Amakuru akoreshwa mu gutuma imikorere ya porogaramu ikora, kubika igenamiterere ry'umukoresha, gutanga impuruza n'ibyibutsa, gukoresha impuruza z'ahantu, kwerekana inyandiko na widget, gukora kopi z'ububiko, gusuzuma amakosa, gukemura ubusabe bw'ubufasha, kunoza ukwizerwa kwa porogaramu, kubahiriza amategeko no kurengera uburenganzira bw'uwakoze porogaramu n'ubw'umukoresha.

Bitewe n'amategeko akurikizwa, ishingiro ryemewe ryo gutunganya amakuru rishobora kuba gushyira mu bikorwa amasezerano y'umukoresha, igikorwa cy'umukoresha, icyemezo kijyanye n'uburenganzira bwihariye, inyungu yemewe mu gutanga ubufasha no kurinda umutekano, no kubahiriza inshingano z'amategeko.

5. Uburenganzira bw'igikoresho

Porogaramu ishobora gusaba gusa uburenganzira bukenewe ku mikorere bireba:

  • imenyesha — ku mpuruza, ibipima gihe, ibyibutsa n'imenyesha rya serivisi;
  • impuruza z'igihe nyacyo n'uburenganzira bw'impuruza — kugira ngo zikore ku gihe cyatoranyijwe n'umukoresha;
  • aho igikoresho giherereye igihe porogaramu ikoreshwa — mu guhitamo ahantu, gukoresha amakarita no gushyiraho impuruza z'ahantu;
  • aho igikoresho giherereye porogaramu ikorera inyuma — ku mpuruza z'ahantu igihe porogaramu idafunguye;
  • mikoro — ku nyandiko z'amajwi, kwinjiza ijwi no kumenya imvugo;
  • interineti — mu gukuramo amatsinda y'indimi, inyandiko z'amategeko, ubufasha, amabwiriza, modeli zo guhindura amajwi inyandiko zikora nta murandasi, amakarita n'imikorere yo ku muyoboro;
  • kugera kuri dosiye binyuze muhitamo-dosiye wa sisitemu — mu kohereza hanze no kwinjiza kopi y'ububiko;
  • igenamiterere rya batiri no gukora inyuma — mu kongera ukwizerwa kw'impuruza, imenyesha n'impuruza z'ahantu.

Umukoresha ashobora gukuraho uburenganzira mu igenamiterere rya Android. Nyuma yo kubukuraho, imikorere imwe ishobora kutaboneka cyangwa gukora ifite imipaka.

6. Aho igikoresho giherereye n'amakarita

Amakuru y'aho igikoresho giherereye akoreshwa mu guhitamo akadomo ku ikarita, kubika impuruza z'ahantu, kumenya kwinjira cyangwa gusohoka mu gice cyagenwe, kwerekana aderesi no gukora ahantu habitswe. Ibipimo by'ahantu n'igenamiterere ry'impuruza z'ahantu bibikwa ku gikoresho keretse umukoresha abyoherereje hanze cyangwa abyoherereje undi.

Iyo umukoresha akoresha amakarita cyangwa gushaka aderesi, porogaramu ishobora kuvugana n'ibice by'amakarita no kumenya aderesi, birimo osmdroid/OpenStreetMap, Nominatim reverse geocoding, Android Geocoder ya sisitemu cyangwa indi serivisi iboneka ku gikoresho. Izo serivisi zishobora kwakira amakuru ya tekiniki y'ubusabe, aderesi IP, ibipimo by'ahantu cyangwa andi makuru akenewe mu kwerekana ikarita cyangwa kumenya aderesi.

7. Amajwi no guhindura imvugo inyandiko nta murandasi cyangwa kuri interineti

Inyandiko z'amajwi n'inyandiko zavanywe mu majwi bikorwa n'igikorwa cy'umukoresha kandi bishobora kuba birimo amakuru bwite cyangwa yihariye iyo umukoresha yayafashe. Bibikwa ku gikoresho keretse umukoresha abyoherereje hanze cyangwa abyoherereje undi.

Guhindura imvugo inyandiko nta murandasi bishobora gukoresha modeli zo kumenya imvugo zakururiwe ku gikoresho. Iyo izo modeli zikururwa, seriveri ya kure ishobora kubona amakuru ya tekiniki y'ihuza, nko kubona aderesi IP n'amakuru y'ubusabe.

Kumenya imvugo kuri interineti bishobora gukoresha serivisi ya Android yo kumenya imvugo yatoranyijwe n'igikoresho, uruganda, umukoresha cyangwa sisitemu y'imikorere. Muri icyo gihe, amakuru y'amajwi n'amakuru ya tekiniki ashobora koherezwa no gutunganywa na serivisi yatoranyijwe yo kumenya imvugo hakurikijwe amategeko yayo. Uwakoze ShiftAlarm ntagenzura politiki y'iyo serivisi ya sisitemu.

8. Ibikururwa ku muyoboro n'amasoko ya kure

Porogaramu ishobora gukoresha interineti mu gukuramo amatsinda y'indimi, dosiye za manifest, inyandiko z'amategeko, ibikoresho by'ubufasha, amabwiriza na modeli zo guhindura imvugo inyandiko zikora nta murandasi, harimo ibikurwa kuri GitHub cyangwa ku rindi soko rya kure ryagenwe muri porogaramu. Muri ubwo busabe, seriveri ya kure ishobora kwakira aderesi IP, itariki n'igihe cy'ubusabe, inzira ya dosiye, amakuru y'ihuza n'andi makuru asanzwe ya tekiniki.

Dosiye porogaramu igenzurira ubusugire zishobora kugenzurwa hakoreshejwe SHA-256 cyangwa indi checksum. Ibi bigabanya ibyago byo kwangirika kwa dosiye ariko ntibyizeza ko isoko rya kure rizahora riboneka.

9. Amaduka ya porogaramu no kwishyura

Porogaramu igurwa mu iduka rya porogaramu, nko muri RuStore cyangwa Google Play, iyo iboneka kuri urwo rubuga. Iduka rya porogaramu ni ryo ryigenga mu gutunganya konti y'umukoresha, kwishyura, gusubizwa amafaranga, amateka y'ibyaguzwe, kuyishyiraho n'amavugurura hakurikijwe amategeko na politiki y'ibanga byaryo.

Uwakoze porogaramu ntiyakira amakarita ya banki, amagambo y'ibanga yo kwishyura cyangwa ibimenyetso byose byo kwishyura igihe kwishyura bikorewe mu iduka rya porogaramu.

10. Kopi z'ububiko no kohereza hanze

Kopi z'ububiko zikorwa gusa n'igikorwa cy'umukoresha. Dosiye ya kopi y'ububiko ishobora kuba irimo igice kinini cy'amakuru abitswe muri porogaramu, harimo impuruza, gahunda, inyandiko, utudomo tw'ahantu, igenamiterere, isuzuma n'andi makuru.

Iyo umukoresha abitse cyangwa asangiye kopi y'ububiko akoresheje indi porogaramu, ububiko bwo mu gicu, imeyili, ubutumwa, umuyobozi wa dosiye cyangwa ububiko bwo hanze, uko iyo dosiye ikomeza kurindwa no gutunganywa bishingira ku buryo bwo kubika cyangwa kohereza yatoranyije.

11. Isuzuma

Inyandiko z'isuzuma zigenewe gushaka no gukemura ibibazo no kugenzura imikorere. Zishobora kuba zirimo amakuru ya tekiniki ku bikorwa bya porogaramu, igihe, uburenganzira, igikoresho, verisiyo ya sisitemu y'imikorere, verisiyo ya porogaramu, imiterere y'impuruza, imiterere y'impuruza z'ahantu, inyandiko, kopi z'ububiko n'amakosa.

Isuzuma ribikwa ku gikoresho kugeza umukoresha arisukuye cyangwa aryoherereje uwakoze porogaramu ku bushake.

12. Gusangira n'abandi

Uwakoze porogaramu ntagurisha amakuru y'umukoresha ku bamamaza kandi ntiyohereza byikora amakuru abitswe muri porogaramu ku bandi. Amakuru ashobora koherezwa iyo umukoresha akoresheje serivisi zo hanze, yohereje ubusabe bw'ubufasha, yohereje kopi y'ububiko hanze, akoresheje amakarita, kumenya aderesi, kumenya imvugo kuri interineti, iduka rya porogaramu cyangwa indi serivisi yo hanze.

Serivisi z'abandi zitunganya amakuru hakurikijwe amategeko yazo. Ni byiza ko umukoresha asoma politiki y'iduka rya porogaramu, serivisi ya imeyili, ububiko bwo mu gicu, serivisi y'amakarita, serivisi yo kumenya imvugo n'izindi serivisi zo hanze yatoranyije.

13. Igihe amakuru abikwa

Amakuru yo ku gikoresho abikwa kugeza umukoresha ayasibye muri porogaramu, asukuye amakuru ya porogaramu, ayikuye ku gikoresho, agaruriye amakuru muri indi kopi y'ububiko cyangwa asibye dosiye ya kopi y'ububiko ahantu yatoranyije kuyibika.

Ubusabe bw'ubufasha, imeyili, imigereka n'ibikoresho by'isuzuma umukoresha yohereje ku wakoze porogaramu bishobora kubikwa nta gihe ntarengwa cyagenwe kugira ngo amateka y'ubufasha abungabungwe, ibibazo bisubiramo bisesengurwe, uburenganzira burindwe kandi hubahirizwe amategeko. Umukoresha ashobora gusaba ko ayo makuru asibwa; ubusabe buzakorwa keretse gukomeza kuyabika bisabwa cyangwa byemewe n'amategeko akurikizwa.

14. Umutekano

Porogaramu ikoresha uburyo busanzwe bwa Android, burimo gutandukanya amakuru ya porogaramu bikorwa na sisitemu y'imikorere, uburenganzira bwa sisitemu n'umuhitamo-dosiye wa sisitemu. Dosiye zimwe zakururwa zishobora kugenzurwa na checksum iyo ubwo buryo bwashyizweho kuri ubwo bwoko bwa dosiye.

Umukoresha ni we ushinzwe kurinda igikoresho, uburyo bwo gufunga ecran, kugera kuri imeyili, serivisi zo mu gicu, kopi z'ububiko n'ububiko bwo hanze. Kopi z'ububiko na dosiye zoherejwe hanze zishobora kugerwaho n'abandi iyo umukoresha azibitse ahatakingiwe cyangwa azisangije abandi.

15. Ibihugu amakuru ashobora gutunganyirizwamo

Amakuru abitswe ku gikoresho aguma ku gikoresho cy'umukoresha mu gihugu gikorerwamo. Amakuru yoherezwa binyuze mu maduka ya porogaramu, GitHub, serivisi z'amakarita, serivisi zo kumenya imvugo, serivisi za imeyili, serivisi zo mu gicu n'izindi serivisi zo hanze ashobora gutunganyirizwa mu bihugu izo serivisi zikoreramo cyangwa zibitsemo ibikorwa remezo.

Amakuru yoherejwe ku bufasha bw'uwakoze porogaramu ashobora gutunganyirizwa aho uwakoze porogaramu akorera cyangwa aho serivisi ze za imeyili, kwakira imbuga no kubika kopi z'ububiko zikorera.

16. Uburenganzira bw'umukoresha

Umukoresha ashobora kureba, guhindura no gusiba amakuru menshi muri porogaramu. Ashobora kandi gusukura amakuru ya porogaramu akoresheje Android, kuyikura ku gikoresho, gukuraho uburenganzira, kohereza kopi y'ububiko hanze cyangwa gusiba dosiye za kopi y'ububiko yikoreye.

Ku makuru yoherejwe ku wakoze porogaramu, umukoresha ashobora kwandikira support@insdeco.ru agasaba kuyabona, kuyakosora, kuyasiba, kugabanya uko atunganywa cyangwa gukuraho icyemezo, aho ubwo burenganzira butangwa n'amategeko. Mu kurinda amakuru, uwakoze porogaramu ashobora gusaba amakuru akenewe kugira ngo yemeze ko ubusabe bujyanye n'umukoresha cyangwa aderesi ya imeyili bireba.

Iyo umukoresha ari mu gihugu cyangwa akarere gatanga uburenganzira bwo gutanga ikirego ku rwego rushinzwe kurengera amakuru, ashobora kwegera urwo rwego hakurikijwe amategeko akurikizwa.

17. Abana

Porogaramu ishobora gushyirwa mu cyiciro cy'imyaka 0+, ariko ntiyagenewe ko uwakoze porogaramu akusanya amakuru bwite y'abana abigambiriye. Iyo umwana akoresha porogaramu, umubyeyi cyangwa umurinzi wemewe n'amategeko agomba kugenzura igenamiterere, uburenganzira, inyandiko, amajwi yafashwe, utudomo tw'ahantu, kopi z'ububiko n'ubusabe bw'ubufasha aho amategeko cyangwa amategeko y'umuryango abisaba.

18. Guhindura iyi Politiki

Uwakoze porogaramu ashobora kuvugurura iyi Politiki. Verisiyo iriho ishobora kuzanwa na porogaramu, gukururwa ku isoko rya kure cyangwa gufungurwa muri kopi iri muri porogaramu nk'iyisimbura. Ku mpinduka zikomeye, porogaramu ishobora gusaba umukoresha gusoma cyangwa kwemera inyandiko yavuguruwe iyo bisabwa n'amategeko cyangwa n'uburyo porogaramu ikora.
