Amasezerano y'imikoreshereze ya ShiftAlarm

Verisiyo 2. Itariki: 2026-06-29.

1. Amabwiriza rusange

Aya Masezerano y'imikoreshereze agenga ikoreshwa rya porogaramu igendanwa ya ShiftAlarm, imikorere yayo, amakuru abikwa ku gikoresho, ibikoresho by'ubufasha, inyandiko z'amategeko n'amavugurura aboneka mu maduka ya porogaramu cyangwa ku masoko ya kure ya porogaramu.

Iyo umukoresha ashyizeho, afunguye cyangwa akoresheje ShiftAlarm, yemeza ko yasomye kandi yemeye aya Masezerano, Politiki y'ibanga n'Itangazo ryo kutaryozwa. Iyo umukoresha atemera izi nyandiko, agomba guhagarika gukoresha porogaramu no kuyikura ku gikoresho.

2. Intego ya porogaramu

ShiftAlarm igenewe gufasha umukoresha gutegura ku giti cye gahunda z'akazi, impuruza zisanzwe, impuruza zishingiye ku gihe cy'akazi, impuruza z'ahantu, inyandiko, ibikorwa, ibyibutsa, inyandiko z'amajwi, inyandiko zavanywe mu majwi, igipima gihe, isaha-mpagarika, amasaha y'isi, widget, kopi z'ububiko no kubara umushahara mu buryo bw'ubufasha.

Porogaramu ni igikoresho cy'ubufasha. Si sisitemu y'umutekano mu nganda, serivisi y'ubuvuzi, ubutabazi bwihutirwa, umutekano, ibaruramari, imisoro, kwishyura imishahara, imicungire y'abakozi cyangwa serivisi y'amategeko.

3. Imyaka n'ubushobozi bwo gukora amasezerano

Porogaramu ishobora gushyirwa mu cyiciro cy'imyaka 0+ mu iduka rya porogaramu, keretse urupapuro rw'iryo duka rubivuze ukundi. Icyo cyiciro gisobanura ko ibikubiye muri porogaramu muri rusange bitagenewe imyaka runaka gusa, ariko ntigisimbura amategeko akurikizwa ku bushobozi bwo gukora amasezerano, ku cyemezo cy'umubyeyi cyangwa ku cyemezo cy'umurinzi wemewe n'amategeko.

Iyo umukoresha adashobora kwikorera amasezerano amubera itegeko cyangwa gutanga ibyemezo bisabwa n'amategeko y'igihugu cye, porogaramu ishobora gukoreshwa gusa habayeho uruhare rw'umubyeyi, umurinzi cyangwa undi muhagarariye mu mategeko.

4. Kwinjira, igikoresho n'uburenganzira

Imikorere imwe ishobora gusaba verisiyo ya Android ihuye na porogaramu, igihe cya sisitemu gishyizweho neza, uburenganzira bw'imenyesha, impuruza z'igihe nyacyo, aho igikoresho giherereye, aho giherereye porogaramu ikorera inyuma, mikoro, interineti, kugera kuri dosiye binyuze muhitamo-dosiye wa sisitemu, n'igenamiterere rya batiri cyangwa gukora inyuma.

Umukoresha ni we ushinzwe gutanga, gukuraho no kugenzura uburenganzira mu igenamiterere rya Android, kandi yumva ko iyo uburenganzira buzimijwe, imikorere imwe ishobora kugira imipaka, gutinda cyangwa kudakora na gato.

5. Kugura n'imikorere yishyurwa

ShiftAlarm itangwa nka porogaramu igurwa rimwe kuri konti y'umukoresha mu iduka rya porogaramu ryakoreshejwe mu kugura, nko muri RuStore cyangwa Google Play, iyo porogaramu iboneka kuri urwo rubuga.

Kwishyura, kwemeza igurwa, gusubizwa amafaranga, kugarura igurwa, gusangira mu muryango, kwimurira ku kindi gikoresho n'ibindi bijyanye na konti bigengwa n'amategeko y'iduka rya porogaramu bireba. Iyo kwishyura bikorewe mu iduka rya porogaramu, uwakoze porogaramu ntiyakira kandi ntatunganya amakarita ya banki, ibimenyetso byo kwishyura cyangwa amagambo y'ibanga ya konti.

Niba mu gihe kizaza hongerwamo ifatabuguzi, ibindi bigurwa cyangwa indi mikorere yishyurwa, aya mabwiriza azavugururwa mbere y'uko bikoreshwa cyangwa mu buryo busabwa n'iduka rya porogaramu bireba.

6. Amakuru abikwa ku gikoresho n'ibikubiyemo by'umukoresha

Umukoresha ni we wikorera, uhindura kandi usiba amakuru muri porogaramu: gahunda z'akazi, impuruza, utudomo tw'ahantu, ibipimo by'ahantu, ahantu habitswe, inyandiko, amajwi yafashwe, inyandiko zavanywe mu majwi, ibarwa ry'umushahara, isuzuma, igenamiterere na kopi z'ububiko.

Umukoresha ni we ushinzwe ko ayo makuru yemewe n'amategeko, ari ukuri, arinzwe kandi ajyanye n'igihe, kandi agomba kubahiriza uburenganzira bw'abandi igihe akora inyandiko, afata amajwi, akora inyandiko zivuye mu majwi cyangwa kopi z'ububiko.

Mu buryo busanzwe amakuru ya porogaramu abikwa ku gikoresho. Iyo umukoresha ayohereje hanze, ayohereje, ayakoporoye cyangwa ayabitse akoresheje indi porogaramu, serivisi yo mu gicu, imeyili, ubutumwa, umuyobozi wa dosiye cyangwa ububiko bwo hanze, uburyo azakomeza gutunganywa bushingira kuri serivisi cyangwa ahantu ho kubika byatoranyijwe n'umukoresha.

7. Ikoreshwa ryemewe

Umukoresha yemera gukoresha porogaramu mu buryo bwemewe n'amategeko gusa, agamije gutegura ku giti cye igihe, ibihe by'akazi, ibyibutsa, impuruza, impuruza z'ahantu, inyandiko n'imikorere bifitanye isano.

Umukoresha ntiyemerewe gukoresha porogaramu mu kugenzura abandi mu buryo bunyuranyije n'amategeko, gukurikirana aho bari mu ibanga, kubangamira uburenganzira bw'ibanga, kurenga ku burenganzira bw'umwanditsi, gukwirakwiza ibikubiyemo byangiza, kurenga ku mbogamizi za tekiniki, kubangamira porogaramu, kuyisesengura inyuma, kuyikuramo kode, kuyihindura, kuyigurisha bundi bushya cyangwa gukwirakwiza kopi zahinduwe, keretse icyo gikorwa cyemewe n'amategeko akurikizwa.

8. Imipaka ku ikoreshwa rikomeye

ShiftAlarm ntiyagenewe kuba igikoresho cyonyine mu bihe ikosa, gutinda, impuruza itabaye, ibarwa ritari ryo cyangwa gutakaza amakuru bishobora guteza ibyago ku buzima, ubuzima bw'umubiri, umutekano, umutungo, ubwikorezi, ibikoresho by'inganda, ibikoresho by'ubuvuzi, sisitemu z'umutekano, serivisi z'ubutabazi, igihe ntarengwa mu mategeko, ingaruka ku kazi cyangwa igihombo kinini cy'amafaranga.

Ku bihe by'akazi by'ingenzi, ingendo, ibikorwa by'ubuvuzi, ibizamini, indege, igihe ntarengwa mu mategeko n'ibindi bikorwa by'ingenzi, umukoresha agomba gukoresha ubundi buryo bwigenga bwo kugenzura n'ibyibutsa.

9. Serivisi n'ibice by'abandi

Ku mikorere imwe, porogaramu ishobora gukorana na serivisi n'ibice bya sisitemu by'abandi, birimo amaduka ya porogaramu, GitHub cyangwa andi masoko ya kure akoreshwa mu gukuramo amatsinda y'indimi, inyandiko z'amategeko, ibikoresho by'ubufasha, amabwiriza na modeli zo guhindura amajwi inyandiko zikora nta murandasi; amakarita ashingiye kuri osmdroid/OpenStreetMap; serivisi zo kumenya aderesi uhereye ku bipimo by'ahantu; Android Geocoder ya sisitemu; serivisi ya Android yo kumenya imvugo; na serivisi zisanzwe za Android z'imenyesha, impuruza, aho igikoresho giherereye, dosiye no gukora inyuma.

Ikoreshwa rya serivisi z'abandi rigengwa n'amabwiriza na politiki byazo. Uwakoze porogaramu ntagenzura serivisi zo hanze, amaduka ya porogaramu, serivisi za sisitemu y'igikoresho, imiyoboro y'itumanaho, seriveri z'amakarita, serivisi zo kumenya imvugo cyangwa serivisi zo mu gicu zatoranyijwe n'umukoresha.

10. Kopi z'ububiko

Kopi z'ububiko zikorwa n'igikorwa cy'umukoresha. Umukoresha ni we ushinzwe gukora kopi z'ububiko, kubika dosiye, kuzirinda abantu batabyemerewe, kugenzura ko kopi ijyanye n'igihe no kugarura amakuru.

Uwakoze porogaramu ntiyizeza ko amakuru azagarurwa muri dosiye ya kopi y'ububiko yangiritse, yahinduwe, ishaje cyangwa itaboneka.

11. Umutungo bwite mu by'ubwenge

Izina rya ShiftAlarm, igishushanyo, imigaragarire, kode nkomoko, imiterere ya porogaramu, inyandiko, amashusho, uburyo ikora, inyandiko ziyisobanura n'ibindi bice bya porogaramu ni iby'uwakoze porogaramu cyangwa bikoreshwa mu buryo bwemewe n'amategeko. Umukoresha ahabwa uburenganzira bugarukira, butihariye kandi budashobora kwimurirwa ku wundi bwo gukoresha porogaramu hakurikijwe aya Masezerano n'amategeko y'iduka rya porogaramu.

Amasomero ya kode ifunguye, amakarita, modeli n'ibindi bice by'abandi bishobora gutangwa hakurikijwe impushya za ba nyirabyo.

12. Ibibazo n'ibirego

Ku mikorere ya porogaramu, inyandiko, ibanga, kugura, amakosa n'ibirego, umukoresha ashobora kwandikira support@insdeco.ru. Ni byiza ko ashyiramo verisiyo ya porogaramu, ubwoko bw'igikoresho, verisiyo ya Android, ibisobanuro by'ikibazo, kandi bibaye ngombwa akongeraho raporo y'isuzuma abona ko yemerewe gusangira.

13. Guhagarika ikoreshwa

Umukoresha ashobora guhagarika gukoresha porogaramu igihe icyo ari cyo cyose ayisiba ku gikoresho cyangwa asukura amakuru yayo. Gusiba porogaramu cyangwa amakuru yayo bishobora gusiba amakuru abitswe ku gikoresho iyo umukoresha atabanje gukora kopi y'ububiko.

Uwakoze porogaramu ashobora guhagarika gushyigikira verisiyo za kera, guhindura uko imikorere imwe iboneka, kuvugurura inyandiko cyangwa guhagarika gutanga porogaramu mu maduka amwe bitewe n'impamvu za tekiniki, amategeko cyangwa imitunganyirize.

14. Guhindura inyandiko

Uwakoze porogaramu ashobora kuvugurura aya Masezerano, Politiki y'ibanga, Itangazo ryo kutaryozwa n'ibikoresho by'ubufasha. Amavugurura ashobora kuzana n'ivugurura rya porogaramu, gukururwa ku isoko rya kure cyangwa gufungurwa muri kopi isanzwe iri muri porogaramu nk'iyisimbura.

Ku mpinduka zikomeye, porogaramu ishobora gusaba umukoresha kongera gusoma cyangwa kwemera inyandiko zavuguruwe. Gukomeza gukoresha porogaramu impinduka zimaze gukurikizwa bisobanura kwemera amabwiriza yavuguruwe, keretse amategeko akurikizwa asaba ubundi buryo.

15. Amategeko akurikizwa n'ingingo za nyuma

Izi nyandiko zateguwe kugira ngo porogaramu ikoreshwe ku rwego mpuzamahanga kandi zigomba gusobanurwa hitawe ku mategeko ategetswe akurikizwa, amategeko y'amaduka ya porogaramu, ibisabwa byo kurengera abaguzi n'ibisabwa byo kurengera amakuru bireba umukoresha cyangwa uwakoze porogaramu. Nta kintu kiri muri izi nyandiko kigabanya uburenganzira bw'umukoresha budashobora kugabanywa n'amategeko ategetswe y'igihugu cye.

Iyo ingingo iyo ari yo yose ifatwa nk'idafite agaciro cyangwa idashobora gushyirwa mu bikorwa, izindi ngingo zikomeza gukurikizwa. Verisiyo z'Ikirusiya n'Icyongereza z'izi nyandiko zigenewe intego imwe; iyo hari ukudahuza, hakurikizwa verisiyo isabwa n'amategeko akurikizwa cyangwa amategeko y'urubuga, kandi iyo nta cyo bisaba, verisiyo y'Ikirusiya ni yo ikurikizwa.
