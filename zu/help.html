Umhlahlandlela womsebenzisi we-ShiftAlarm

Inguqulo 1. Usuku: 2026-06-23.

1. Ukuqalisa kokuqala

Vula uhlelo lokusebenza, khetha ulimi oludingekayo, hlola izimvume bese ulungiselela ukwethembeka kwama-alamu. Kwamanye amadivayisi kungase kudingeke futhi uvumele umsebenzi wangemuva futhi ususe imikhawulo yebhethri.

2. Ama-alamu

Dala ama-alamu avamile, ama-alamu aphindaphindwa ngezinsuku, ama-alamu osuku oluthile nama-alamu axhunywe kumashifu. Ngama-alamu abalulekile, hlola izaziso, ama-alamu anembile nemikhawulo yebhethri.

3. Ama-alamu endawo

Dala i-alamu yendawo emephini ngokucindezela isikhathi eside. Ukuze ithembeke kangcono, sebenzisa iradiyasi engu-800–1000 m futhi uvumele indawo enembile nendawo yangemuva.

4. Amashifu

Dala ishejuli yamashifu esebenzayo. Ama-alamu axhunywe kumashifu namawijethi ekhalenda asebenzisa ishejuli esebenzayo futhi acabangela izinguquko zezinsuku.

5. Amanothi nezikhumbuzi

Sebenzisa amanothi ombhalo, izinhlu, imicimbi, izikhumbuzi, amanothi omsindo nokuguqula izwi libe umbhalo. Amamodeli enkulumo angaxhunyiwe ku-inthanethi angalandwa ezilungiselelweni.

6. Amawijethi

Engeza iwijethi ethi Namuhla noma iwijethi Yekhalenda esikrinini sasekhaya se-Android. Amawijethi abonisa ulwazi oluseduze futhi azibuyekeza ngokuzenzakalelayo; kukhona nenkinobho yokuvuselela mathupha.

7. Amakhophi ayisipele

Ezilungiselelweni ungagcina ikhophi eyisipele efonini noma wabelane ngayo ngephaneli yokwabelana ye-Android.

8. Ukuxilonga

Uma umsebenzi ungasebenzi kahle, vula ilogi yokuxilonga ehambisanayo, uyikopishe noma uyithumele kumthuthukisi kanye nencazelo yenkinga.
