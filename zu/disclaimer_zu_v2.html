Isitatimende sokuzihlangula se-ShiftAlarm

Inguqulo 2. Usuku: 2026-06-29.

I-ShiftAlarm iwuhlelo lokusebenza olusizayo lokuhlela amashifu, ama-alamu, ama-alamu endawo, amanothi, izikhumbuzi, isibali sikhathi, isitophuwashi, amawijethi, amakhophi ayisipele nemisebenzi ehlobene. Uhlelo lokusebenza alulona uhlelo oluqinisekisiwe lwezokuphepha, lwezaziso zezimo eziphuthumayo, lokuqapha kwezokwelapha, lwe-HR, lwezimali noma lokukhokhelwa kwemiholo.

1. Uhlelo lokusebenza lunikezwa “njengoba lunjalo”

Uhlelo lokusebenza lunikezwa “njengoba lunjalo” futhi “njengoba lutholakala”. Umthuthukisi uthatha izinyathelo ezifanele ukuze alugcine luzinzile, kodwa akaqinisekisi ukuthi yonke imisebenzi izosebenza ngaphandle kwamaphutha, ukubambezeleka, izehlakalo ezingaqali, ukungahambisani, ukulahleka kwedatha noma imikhawulo kuwo wonke amadivayisi, izinguqulo ze-Android, i-firmware yomkhiqizi nezimo zokusetshenziswa.

2. Asikho isiqinisekiso esiphelele sokulethwa

Ama-alamu, ama-alamu axhunywe kumashifu, ama-alamu endawo, izibali sikhathi, izikhumbuzi, izaziso namawijethi kuncika ku-Android, kumkhiqizi wedivayisi, ezimvumeni, esikhathini sesistimu esilungile, kumodi yokonga ibhethri, emikhawulweni yomsebenzi wangemuva, esimweni sebhethri, kumodi ethi Do Not Disturb, ezilungiselelweni zezaziso, ekuqalisweni kabusha, ezibuyekezweni zesistimu yokusebenza, ekuvalweni kohlelo lokusebenza mathupha, emaphutheni esistimu nasezenzweni zomsebenzisi.

Ukulethwa kungabambezeleka, kungaphuthelwa, kwenzeke ngesikhathi esihlukile kunesilindelwe, kwenzeke ngaphandle komsindo noma isaziso, noma kukhawulelwe yisistimu.

3. Imicimbi ebalulekile nokusetshenziswa ezimweni ezibucayi

Ungasebenzisi i-ShiftAlarm njengendlela kuphela yokulawula imicimbi ebucayi. Ngamashifu abalulekile, uhambo, izinqubo zezokwelapha, izivivinyo, izindiza, izikhathi ezinqunyiwe zomthetho, imicimbi ebalulekile ngokwezimali neminye imisebenzi ebalulekile, sebenzisa izikhumbuzi nokuhlola okwengeziwe okuzimele.

Uhlelo lokusebenza akumele lusetshenziswe njengengxenye yamasistimu lapho iphutha noma ukubambezeleka kungalimaza ukuphila, impilo, ukuphepha, impahla, ezokuthutha, imishini yezimboni, amadivayisi ezokwelapha, amasistimu ezokuphepha noma ukusabela ezimweni eziphuthumayo.

4. Ama-alamu endawo nendawo

Ama-alamu endawo ancike ku-GPS, Wi-Fi, amanethiwekhi eselula, ukunemba kwendawo, ukusetshenziswa ngaphakathi, ukuminyana kwezakhiwo, izimo zezulu, indawo yangemuva, imikhawulo yesistimu ye-Android, izilungiselelo zebhethri, iradiyasi ye-geofence, ikhwalithi yemephu nokutholakala kwezinsizakalo zangaphandle.

I-alamu yendawo ingaqala sekwephuzile, ingaqali, iqale ngokunganembile noma ikhawulelwe yisistimu. Ama-alamu endawo awahloselwe ukulandelela abantu ngokufihlekile, ukuqapha abasebenzi, imisebenzi yezokuphepha, izaziso zezimo eziphuthumayo noma imisebenzi lapho iphutha lendawo lingadala umonakalo.

5. Amamephu namakheli

Amamephu, amakheli, izixhumanisi zendawo nemiphumela yokuthola ikheli ngezixhumanisi zendawo kungase kungaphelele, kuphelelwe yisikhathi, kunganembi noma kungatholakali. Ikheli elitholwe ngezixhumanisi zendawo liwulwazi olusizayo futhi alisona isiqinisekiso sendawo esinembile ngokomthetho.

6. Umsindo nemibhalo eguqulwe isuselwa enkulumweni

Ukubona inkulumo, okuhlanganisa ukuguqula inkulumo ungaxhunyiwe noma uxhumeke ku-inthanethi, kungaba namaphutha, okushiyiwe, ukubonwa okungalungile kwamagama, izinombolo, izinsuku, amatemu, izilimi nemisho. Umsebenzisi kufanele azihlolele ngokuzimela amarekhodi abalulekile.

Okurekhodiwe komsindo nemibhalo eguqulwe isuselwa enkulumweni akumele kusetshenziswe njengomthombo okuwukuphela kwawo wolwazi olubalulekile ngokomthetho, lwezokwelapha, lwezimali, lwe-HR noma olunye ulwazi olubucayi.

7. Umholo nezibalo

Imisebenzi yomholo, amabhonasi, ukudonswa, izinhlawulo, iziphindaphindi zamashifu nemibiko iyasiza futhi ingeyolwazi kuphela. Ayisona iseluleko sezimali, sentela, sezomthetho noma se-HR.

Umsebenzisi kufanele azihlolele ngokuzimela izibalo ngokuzifanisa namadokhumenti asemthethweni omqashi, izivumelwano zokuqashwa, iziliphu zomholo, imithetho yentela nomthetho osebenzayo.

8. Amakhophi ayisipele nokulahleka kwedatha

Umthuthukisi akaqinisekisi ukugcinwa kwedatha uma umsebenzisi engazange adale ikhophi eyisipele, elahlekelwe yifayela, eligcine endaweni engatholakali, esuse idatha yohlelo lokusebenza, esule isitoreji, ashintshe amadivayisi, asebenzise ifayela elonakele, avale izimvume ezidingekayo noma abelane ngefayela nezinhlangano zangaphandle.

Amakhophi ayisipele angaqukatha ulwazi lomuntu siqu. Umsebenzisi unesibopho sokuwagcina, ukuwadlulisa, ukuwavikela nokuwasusa.

9. Izinsizakalo zezinhlangano zangaphandle

Eminye imisebenzi ingancika ezitolo zezinhlelo zokusebenza, ku-GitHub noma kweminye imithombo ekude, ku-OpenStreetMap/osmdroid, ezinsizakalweni zokuthola ikheli ngezixhumanisi zendawo, ensizakalweni yesistimu ye-Android yokubona inkulumo, ezinsizakalweni ze-imeyili, kusitoreji samafu, kusistimu yokusebenza nakumkhiqizi wedivayisi.

Umthuthukisi akanacala ngokutholakala, ukunemba, ukuvikeleka, imithetho, izinguquko, ukwehluleka, imikhawulo noma izenzo zezinsizakalo zezinhlangano zangaphandle ezikhethwe ngumsebenzisi noma ezinikezwa yisistimu yokusebenza.

10. Izenzo zomsebenzisi

Umsebenzisi unesibopho sokusetha kahle uhlelo lokusebenza, amashejuli, ama-alamu, ama-geofence, izimvume, izaziso, ibhethri, umsebenzi wangemuva, amakhophi ayisipele, isikhathi sesistimu kanye nokuhlola ukwethembeka kwemisebenzi kudivayisi yakhe.

11. Ukukhawulelwa kwesibopho

Ngokwezinga eliphezulu elivunyelwe ngumthetho osebenzayo, umthuthukisi akanacala ngomonakalo ongaqondile, inzuzo elahlekile, izinhlawulo, imiphumela yokuqondiswa emsebenzini, amashifu aphuthelwe, ukufika sekwephuzile, ukulahleka kwezimali, ukulahleka kwedatha, izibalo ezingalungile, amaphutha omsebenzisi, ukwehluleka kwedivayisi, imikhawulo ye-Android, izenzo zabakhiqizi bamadivayisi, amanethiwekhi okuxhumana, izitolo zezinhlelo zokusebenza noma izinsizakalo zezinhlangano zangaphandle.

Akukho kuleli dokhumenti okunciphisa amalungelo omsebenzisi angenakuncishiswa ngaphansi komthetho osebenzayo obophezelayo.

12. Amadokhumenti ahlobene

Lesi Sitatimende sokuzihlangula sisebenza kanye neMigomo yokusebenzisa ye-ShiftAlarm kanye neNqubomgomo yobumfihlo. Uma umsebenzisi engavumelani nale mikhawulo nalezi zingozi, kufanele ayeke ukusebenzisa uhlelo lokusebenza.
