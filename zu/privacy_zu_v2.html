Inqubomgomo yobumfihlo ye-ShiftAlarm

Inguqulo 2. Usuku: 2026-06-29.

1. Umgomo ovamile wokucutshungulwa kwedatha

I-ShiftAlarm ngokuyinhloko isebenza ngemodeli ethi offline-first. Idatha eyinhloko yomsebenzisi iyadalwa, iyacutshungulwa futhi igcinwe endaweni kudivayisi yomsebenzisi. Umthuthukisi akayitholi le datha ngokuzenzakalelayo, ngaphandle uma umsebenzisi eyithumela ngesicelo sosekelo, ngokuthumela ngaphandle, nge-imeyili, ngohlelo lwemiyalezo, ngesitoreji samafu, ngesitolo sezinhlelo zokusebenza noma ngenye indlela ekhethwe ngumsebenzisi.

Kule nguqulo, uhlelo lokusebenza alusebenzisi amanethiwekhi okukhangisa, uhlelo lwalo lwama-akhawunti, ukugunyazwa ohlangothini lweseva, i-Firebase Analytics, i-Google Analytics, i-Crashlytics noma i-AdMob, ngaphandle uma lokhu kushiwo ngokusobala enguqulweni ebuyekeziwe yale Nqubomgomo.

2. Idatha engagcinwa endaweni

Kuye ngemisebenzi esetshenziswayo, uhlelo lokusebenza lungagcina kudivayisi le datha elandelayo:

  • izilungiselelo zohlelo lokusebenza, ulimi, itimu, izilungiselelo zamawijethi nokuncanyelwayo ngumsebenzisi;
  • ama-alamu avamile, ama-alamu axhunywe kumashifu, isikhathi, ukuphinda, imisindo, ukudlidliza, ukuhlehlisa, ukukhuphuka kancane komsindo nezilungiselelo ezihlobene;
  • amashejuli amashifu, izifanekiso zamashifu, izinguquko zekhalenda, izinsuku zokuphumula nezimpawu zomsebenzisi;
  • ama-alamu endawo: izixhumanisi zendawo, iradiyasi, uhlobo lwesenzakalo sokungena noma ukuphuma, ikheli, igama lendawo, izindawo ezilondoloziwe, ishejuli nesimo sokuqalisa;
  • amanothi, imicimbi, izikhumbuzi, izinhlu zokuhlola, izingxenye zombhalo nezinsuku ezihlobene;
  • amanothi omsindo, okurekhodiwe kwezwi nemibhalo eguqulwe isuselwa enkulumweni;
  • isibali sikhathi, isitophuwashi, amawashi omhlaba nomlando wezenzo ezithile lapho lowo mlando uyingxenye yomsebenzi;
  • izilungiselelo zokubala umholo, amabhonasi, ukudonswa, izinhlawulo, imibiko nezibalo ezihlobene;
  • amalogi okuxilonga, izehlakalo zobuchwepheshe, isimo sezimvume, amaphutha nezimo zemisebenzi;
  • amakhophi ayisipele uma umsebenzisi ewadala mathupha.

3. Idatha umsebenzisi angayithumela kumthuthukisi

Uma umsebenzisi exhumana nosekelo ku-support@insdeco.ru, umthuthukisi angathola ikheli le-imeyili lomsebenzisi, igama noma isiginesha ye-imeyili, umbhalo womlayezo, okunamathiselwe, izithombe-skrini, amafayela amakhophi ayisipele, imibiko yokuxilonga, imodeli yedivayisi, inguqulo ye-Android, inguqulo yohlelo lokusebenza, isikhathi sesenzakalo kanye nencazelo yenkinga.

Le datha ithunyelwa kuphela ngesenzo somsebenzisi. Umsebenzisi kufanele ahlole i-imeyili nokunamathiselwe ngaphambi kokukuthumela.

4. Izinhloso zokucubungula

Idatha isetshenziselwa ukwenza imisebenzi yohlelo lokusebenza, ukugcina izilungiselelo zomsebenzisi, ukuletha ama-alamu nezikhumbuzi, ukusebenzisa ama-alamu endawo, ukubonisa amanothi namawijethi, ukudala amakhophi ayisipele, ukuhlonza amaphutha, ukucubungula izicelo zosekelo, ukuthuthukisa ukuzinza kohlelo lokusebenza, ukuhambisana nezidingo zomthetho kanye nokuvikela amalungelo omthuthukisi nawomsebenzisi.

Kuye ngomthetho osebenzayo, izisekelo zomthetho zokucubungula zingabandakanya ukufeza isivumelwano somsebenzisi, isenzo somsebenzisi, imvume yezimvume ezithile, intshisekelo esemthethweni ekusekeleni nasekuvikelekeni, kanye nokugcwaliswa kwezibopho zomthetho.

5. Izimvume zedivayisi

Uhlelo lokusebenza lungacela kuphela izimvume ezidingekayo emisebenzini efanele:

  • izaziso — zama-alamu, izibali sikhathi, izikhumbuzi nezaziso zesevisi;
  • ama-alamu anembile nezimvume zama-alamu — ukuze ama-alamu aqale ngesikhathi esikhethwe ngumsebenzisi;
  • indawo ngesikhathi sokusebenzisa uhlelo lokusebenza — ukukhetha izindawo, ukusebenzisa amamephu nokusetha ama-alamu endawo;
  • indawo yangemuva — ukuze ama-alamu endawo asebenze lapho uhlelo lokusebenza lungavuliwe;
  • imakrofoni — yamanothi omsindo, ukufaka ngezwi nokubona inkulumo;
  • i-inthanethi — ukulanda amaphakheji ezilimi, amadokhumenti omthetho, usizo, imiyalelo, amamodeli okuguqula inkulumo abe umbhalo angaxhunyiwe ku-inthanethi, amamephu nemisebenzi yenethiwekhi;
  • ukufinyelela kumafayela ngesikhethi sefayela sesistimu — ukuthumela ngaphandle nokungenisa ikhophi eyisipele;
  • izilungiselelo zebhethri nomsebenzi wangemuva — ukuthuthukisa ukwethembeka kwama-alamu, izaziso nama-alamu endawo.

Umsebenzisi angahoxisa izimvume ezilungiselelweni ze-Android. Ngemva kokuhoxiswa, eminye imisebenzi ingase ingatholakali noma isebenze ngokulinganiselwe.

6. Indawo namamephu

Idatha yendawo isetshenziselwa ukukhetha iphuzu emephini, ukugcina ama-alamu endawo, ukuthola ukungena noma ukuphuma endaweni echaziwe, ukubonisa ikheli nokudala izindawo ezilondoloziwe. Izixhumanisi zendawo nezilungiselelo zama-alamu endawo zigcinwa endaweni kudivayisi, ngaphandle uma umsebenzisi ezithumela ngaphandle noma ezithumela.

Lapho umsebenzisi esebenzisa amamephu noma ukusesha ikheli, uhlelo lokusebenza lungaxhumana nezingxenye zamamephu nezokuthola ikheli ngezixhumanisi zendawo, okuhlanganisa i-osmdroid/OpenStreetMap, i-Nominatim reverse geocoding, i-Android Geocoder yesistimu noma enye insizakalo etholakala kudivayisi. Lezi zinsizakalo zingathola idatha yesicelo sobuchwepheshe, ikheli le-IP, izixhumanisi zendawo noma enye idatha edingekayo ukubonisa imephu noma ukuthola ikheli.

7. Umsindo nokuguqula inkulumo ibe umbhalo ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi noma ku-inthanethi

Amanothi omsindo nemibhalo eguqulwe isuselwa enkulumweni kudalwa ngesenzo somsebenzisi futhi kungaba nolwazi lomuntu siqu noma olubucayi uma umsebenzisi elurekhoda. Lolu lwazi lugcinwa endaweni kudivayisi, ngaphandle uma umsebenzisi eluthumela ngaphandle noma eluthumela.

Ukuguqula inkulumo ibe umbhalo ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi kungase kusebenzise amamodeli okubona inkulumo alandwe endaweni. Lapho lawo mamodeli elandwa, iseva ekude ingabona idatha yobuchwepheshe yokuxhumeka, njengekheli le-IP nolwazi lwesicelo.

Ukubona inkulumo ku-inthanethi kungase kusebenzise insizakalo yesistimu ye-Android yokubona inkulumo ekhethwe yidivayisi, umkhiqizi, umsebenzisi noma isistimu yokusebenza. Kulokhu, idatha yomsindo nolwazi lobuchwepheshe kungathunyelwa futhi kucutshungulwe yinsizakalo yokubona inkulumo ekhethiwe ngaphansi kwemithetho yayo. Umthuthukisi we-ShiftAlarm akalawuli izinqubomgomo zaleyo nsizakalo yesistimu.

8. Ukulanda ngenethiwekhi nemithombo ekude

Uhlelo lokusebenza lungasebenzisa i-inthanethi ukulanda amaphakheji ezilimi, amafayela e-manifest, amadokhumenti omthetho, izinto zosizo, imiyalelo namamodeli okuguqula inkulumo abe umbhalo angaxhunyiwe ku-inthanethi, okuhlanganisa ukulanda ku-GitHub noma komunye umthombo okude ochazwe ohlelweni lokusebenza. Phakathi nalezi zicelo, iseva ekude ingathola ikheli le-IP, usuku nesikhathi sesicelo, indlela yefayela, ulwazi lokuxhumeka nenye idatha yobuchwepheshe ejwayelekile.

Amafayela anezindlela zokuhlola ubuqotho ezisetshenzisiwe ohlelweni lokusebenza angahlolwa nge-SHA-256 noma elinye inani lokuhlola. Lokhu kunciphisa ingozi yokonakala kwefayela kodwa akuqinisekisi ukutholakala komthombo okude.

9. Izitolo zezinhlelo zokusebenza nezinkokhelo

Uhlelo lokusebenza luthengwa ngesitolo sezinhlelo zokusebenza, njenge-RuStore noma i-Google Play, uma lutholakala kuleyo nkundla. Isitolo sezinhlelo zokusebenza sicubungula ngokuzimela i-akhawunti yomsebenzisi, izinkokhelo, ukubuyiselwa kwemali, umlando wokuthenga, ukufakwa nezibuyekezo ngaphansi kwemithetho yaso nenqubomgomo yaso yobumfihlo.

Umthuthukisi akatholi amakhadi asebhange, amaphasiwedi okukhokha noma imininingwane ephelele yokukhokha lapho inkokhelo yenziwa ngesitolo sezinhlelo zokusebenza.

10. Amakhophi ayisipele nokuthumela ngaphandle

Amakhophi ayisipele adalwa kuphela ngesenzo somsebenzisi. Ifayela lekhophi eyisipele lingaba nengxenye enkulu yedatha yasendaweni yohlelo lokusebenza, okuhlanganisa ama-alamu, amashejuli, amanothi, amaphuzu endawo, izilungiselelo, ukuxilonga nolunye ulwazi.

Uma umsebenzisi egcina noma abelana ngekhophi eyisipele ngolunye uhlelo lokusebenza, isitoreji samafu, i-imeyili, uhlelo lwemiyalezo, imenenja yamafayela noma isitoreji sangaphandle, ukuvikelwa nokucutshungulwa okuqhubekayo kwalelo fayela kuncike endleleni yokugcina noma yokudlulisa ekhethwe ngumsebenzisi.

11. Ukuxilonga

Amalogi okuxilonga ahloselwe ukuxazulula izinkinga nokuhlola imisebenzi. Angaba nolwazi lobuchwepheshe mayelana nezehlakalo zohlelo lokusebenza, isikhathi, izimvume, idivayisi, inguqulo yesistimu yokusebenza, inguqulo yohlelo lokusebenza, isimo se-alamu, isimo se-alamu yendawo, amanothi, amakhophi ayisipele namaphutha.

Ukuxilonga kugcinwa endaweni kuze kube yilapho umsebenzisi ekusula noma ekuthumela mathupha kumthuthukisi.

12. Ukwabelana nezinhlangano zangaphandle

Umthuthukisi akayithengisi idatha yomsebenzisi kubakhangisi futhi akayidluliseli ngokuzenzakalelayo idatha yasendaweni yohlelo lokusebenza ezinhlanganweni zangaphandle. Idatha ingadluliselwa uma umsebenzisi esebenzisa izinsizakalo zangaphandle, ethumela isicelo sosekelo, ethumela ngaphandle ikhophi eyisipele, esebenzisa amamephu, ukuthola ikheli ngezixhumanisi zendawo, ukubona inkulumo ku-inthanethi, isitolo sezinhlelo zokusebenza noma enye insizakalo yangaphandle.

Izinsizakalo zezinhlangano zangaphandle zicubungula idatha ngaphansi kwemithetho yazo. Kunconywa ukuthi umsebenzisi abuyekeze izinqubomgomo zesitolo sezinhlelo zokusebenza, insizakalo ye-imeyili, isitoreji samafu, insizakalo yamamephu, insizakalo yokubona inkulumo nezinye izinsizakalo zangaphandle azikhethayo.

13. Isikhathi sokugcina idatha

Idatha yasendaweni igcinwa kudivayisi kuze kube yilapho umsebenzisi eyisusa ohlelweni lokusebenza, esula idatha yohlelo lokusebenza, esusa uhlelo lokusebenza, ebuyisela idatha esuka kwenye ikhophi eyisipele noma esusa ifayela lekhophi eyisipele endaweni yokugcina ekhethiwe.

Izicelo zosekelo, ama-imeyili, okunamathiselwe nezinto zokuxilonga ezithunyelwe ngumsebenzisi kumthuthukisi zingagcinwa ngaphandle kwesikhathi sokuphelelwa esinqunywe kusengaphambili ukuze kugcinwe umlando wosekelo, kuhlaziywe izinkinga eziphindaphindayo, kuvikelwe amalungelo futhi kugcwaliswe izibopho zomthetho. Umsebenzisi angacela ukusulwa kwaleyo datha; isicelo sizogcwaliswa ngaphandle uma ukuqhubeka nokuyigcina kudingwa noma kuvunyelwa ngumthetho osebenzayo.

14. Ukuvikeleka

Uhlelo lokusebenza lusebenzisa izindlela ezijwayelekile ze-Android, okuhlanganisa ukuhlukaniswa kwedatha yohlelo lokusebenza yisistimu yokusebenza, izimvume zesistimu nesikhethi sefayela sesistimu. Amafayela athile alandiwe angahlolwa ngenani lokuhlola uma lokho kuhlolwa kwenziwe kulolo hlobo lwefayela.

Umsebenzisi unesibopho sokuvikela idivayisi, ukukhiya isikrini, ukufinyelela ku-imeyili, izinsizakalo zamafu, amakhophi ayisipele nesitoreji sangaphandle. Amakhophi ayisipele namafayela athunyelwe ngaphandle angafinyelelwa ngabanye abantu uma umsebenzisi ewagcina endaweni engavikelekile noma ewabelana nezinhlangano zangaphandle.

15. Amazwe lapho idatha icutshungulwa khona

Idatha yasendaweni ihlala kudivayisi yomsebenzisi ezweni lapho idivayisi isetshenziswa khona. Idatha edluliswa ngezitolo zezinhlelo zokusebenza, i-GitHub, izinsizakalo zamamephu, izinsizakalo zokubona inkulumo, izinsizakalo ze-imeyili, izinsizakalo zamafu nezinye izinsizakalo zangaphandle ingacutshungulwa emazweni lapho lezo zinsizakalo zisebenza khona noma zigcina khona ingqalasizinda.

Idatha ethunyelwa kusekelo lomthuthukisi ingacutshungulwa lapho umthuthukisi nezinsizakalo zakhe ze-imeyili, zokusingatha noma zamakhophi ayisipele zitholakala khona.

16. Amalungelo omsebenzisi

Umsebenzisi angabuka, aguqule futhi asuse iningi ledatha ngqo ohlelweni lokusebenza. Angaphinda asule idatha yohlelo lokusebenza nge-Android, asuse uhlelo lokusebenza, ahoxise izimvume, athumele ngaphandle ikhophi eyisipele noma asuse amafayela amakhophi ayisipele awadalile.

Mayelana nedatha ethunyelwe kumthuthukisi, umsebenzisi angaxhumana no-support@insdeco.ru futhi acele ukufinyelela, ukulungiswa, ukusulwa, ukukhawulelwa kokucutshungulwa noma ukuhoxiswa kwemvume lapho lawo malungelo esebenza ngokomthetho. Ukuvikela idatha, umthuthukisi angacela ulwazi oludingekayo ukuqinisekisa ukuthi isicelo sihlobene nomsebenzisi noma ikheli le-imeyili elifanele.

Uma umsebenzisi esendaweni enomthetho onikeza ilungelo lokufaka isikhalo kusiphathimandla sokuvikelwa kwedatha, angaxhumana naleso siphathimandla ngaphansi komthetho osebenzayo.

17. Izingane

Uhlelo lokusebenza lungaba nesilinganiso seminyaka esingu-0+, kodwa aluhloselwe ukuqoqwa okuqondiswe kwedatha yomuntu siqu yezingane ngumthuthukisi. Uma ingane isebenzisa uhlelo lokusebenza, umzali noma umnakekeli osemthethweni kufanele aqondise izilungiselelo, izimvume, amanothi, okurekhodiwe komsindo, amaphuzu endawo, amakhophi ayisipele nezicelo zosekelo lapho kudingwa ngumthetho noma yimithetho yomndeni.

18. Izinguquko kule Nqubomgomo

Umthuthukisi angabuyekeza le Nqubomgomo. Inguqulo yamanje ingalethwa nohlelo lokusebenza, ilandwe emthonjeni okude noma ivulwe enguqulweni eyisipele efakwe ngaphakathi kohlelo lokusebenza. Ngezinguquko ezibalulekile, uhlelo lokusebenza lungacela umsebenzisi ukuthi abuyekeze noma amukele idokhumenti ebuyekeziwe uma kudingwa ngumthetho noma indlela yokusebenza yohlelo lokusebenza.
