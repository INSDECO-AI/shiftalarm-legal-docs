Imigomo yokusebenzisa ye-ShiftAlarm

Inguqulo 2. Usuku: 2026-06-29.

1. Imigomo evamile

Le Migomo yokusebenzisa ilawula ukusetshenziswa kohlelo lokusebenza lweselula i-ShiftAlarm, imisebenzi yalo, idatha yasendaweni, izinto zosizo, amadokhumenti omthetho kanye nezibuyekezo ezitholakala ngezitolo zezinhlelo zokusebenza noma ngemithombo ekude yohlelo lokusebenza.

Ngokufaka, ukuvula noma ukusebenzisa i-ShiftAlarm, umsebenzisi uqinisekisa ukuthi ufundile futhi wamukela le Migomo, Inqubomgomo yobumfihlo kanye neSitatimende sokuzihlangula. Uma umsebenzisi engavumelani nala madokhumenti, kufanele ayeke ukusebenzisa uhlelo lokusebenza futhi alususe kudivayisi.

2. Inhloso yohlelo lokusebenza

I-ShiftAlarm ihloselwe ukuhlela komuntu siqu amashejuli amashifu, ama-alamu avamile, ama-alamu axhunywe kumashifu, ama-alamu endawo, amanothi, imicimbi, izikhumbuzi, amanothi omsindo, imibhalo eguqulwe isuselwa enkulumweni, isibali sikhathi, isitophuwashi, amawashi omhlaba, amawijethi, amakhophi ayisipele kanye nezibalo zomholo ezisizayo.

Uhlelo lokusebenza luyithuluzi elisizayo. Alulona uhlelo lokuphepha kwezimboni, insizakalo yezokwelapha, yezimo eziphuthumayo, yezokuphepha, yezimali, yentela, yokukhokhelwa kwemiholo, ye-HR noma yezomthetho.

3. Iminyaka nekhono lokwenza izenzo zomthetho

Uhlelo lokusebenza lungaba nesilinganiso seminyaka esingu-0+ esitolo sezinhlelo zokusebenza, ngaphandle uma ikhasi lesitolo lisho okuhlukile. Lesi silinganiso sisho ukuthi okuqukethwe kohlelo lokusebenza ngokuvamile akunqunyelwe ngokweminyaka, kodwa asishintshi imithetho esebenzayo ephathelene nekhono lokwenza izenzo zomthetho, imvume yomzali noma imvume yomnakekeli osemthethweni.

Uma umsebenzisi engakwazi ukuzimela ekungeneni ezivumelwaneni ezibophezela ngokomthetho noma ekunikezeni izimvume ezidingekayo ngokwemithetho yezwe lakhe, uhlelo lokusebenza lungasetshenziswa kuphela ngokubandakanywa komzali, umnakekeli noma omunye ummeleli osemthethweni.

4. Ukufinyelela, idivayisi nezimvume

Eminye imisebenzi ingadinga inguqulo ehambisanayo ye-Android, isikhathi sesistimu esilungile, izimvume zezaziso, ama-alamu anembile, indawo, indawo yangemuva, imakrofoni, i-inthanethi, ukufinyelela kumafayela ngesikhethi sefayela sesistimu, kanye nezilungiselelo zebhethri nomsebenzi wangemuva.

Umsebenzisi unesibopho sokunikeza, ukuhoxisa nokuhlola izimvume ezilungiselelweni ze-Android futhi uyaqonda ukuthi uma izimvume zivaliwe, eminye imisebenzi ingase isebenze ngokulinganiselwe, ibambezeleke noma ingasebenzi nhlobo.

5. Ukuthenga nemisebenzi ekhokhelwayo

I-ShiftAlarm isatshalaliswa njengohlelo lokusebenza oluthengwa kanye kuphela ku-akhawunti yomsebenzisi esitolo sezinhlelo zokusebenza lapho ukuthenga kwenziwa khona, njenge-RuStore noma i-Google Play, uma uhlelo lokusebenza lutholakala kuleyo nkundla.

Inkokhelo, ukuqinisekiswa kokuthenga, ukubuyiselwa kwemali, ukubuyiselwa kokuthenga, ukwabelana komndeni, ukudlulisela kwenye idivayisi kanye nezinye izindaba ze-akhawunti kulawulwa yimithetho yesitolo sezinhlelo zokusebenza esifanele. Umthuthukisi akatholi futhi akacubunguli amakhadi asebhange, imininingwane yokukhokha noma amaphasiwedi e-akhawunti lapho inkokhelo yenziwa ngesitolo sezinhlelo zokusebenza.

Uma okubhaliselwe, ukuthenga okwengeziwe noma eminye imisebenzi ekhokhelwayo kwengezwa esikhathini esizayo, imigomo izobuyekezwa ngaphambi kokuthi leyo misebenzi isetshenziswe noma ngendlela edingwa yisitolo sezinhlelo zokusebenza esifanele.

6. Idatha yasendaweni nokuqukethwe ngumsebenzisi

Umsebenzisi uzidalela, aziguqule futhi azisuse ngokwakhe idatha ohlelweni lokusebenza: amashejuli amashifu, ama-alamu, amaphuzu endawo, izixhumanisi zendawo, izindawo ezilondoloziwe, amanothi, okurekhodiwe komsindo, imibhalo eguqulwe isuselwa enkulumweni, izibalo zomholo, ukuxilonga, izilungiselelo namakhophi ayisipele.

Umsebenzisi unesibopho sokuba leyo datha isemthethweni, inembile, ivikelekile futhi ihambisane nenhloso, kanye nokuhlonipha amalungelo ezinhlangano zangaphandle lapho edala amanothi, okurekhodiwe komsindo, imibhalo eguqulwe isuselwa enkulumweni namakhophi ayisipele.

Ngokuzenzakalelayo, idatha yohlelo lokusebenza igcinwa endaweni kudivayisi. Uma umsebenzisi ethumela ngaphandle, ethumela, ekopisha noma egcina idatha ngolunye uhlelo lokusebenza, insizakalo yamafu, i-imeyili, uhlelo lwemiyalezo, imenenja yamafayela noma isitoreji sangaphandle, ukucutshungulwa okuqhubekayo kuncike ensizakalweni noma endaweni yokugcina ekhethwe ngumsebenzisi.

7. Ukusetshenziswa okuvunyelwe

Umsebenzisi uyavuma ukusebenzisa uhlelo lokusebenza ngokusemthethweni kuphela, ukuze ahlele isikhathi sakhe, amashifu, izikhumbuzi, ama-alamu, ama-alamu endawo, amanothi nemisebenzi ehlobene.

Umsebenzisi akumele asebenzise uhlelo lokusebenza ukuqapha abanye abantu ngokungemthetho, ukulandelela indawo ngokufihlekile, ukwephula amalungelo obumfihlo, ukwephula ilungelo lobunikazi, ukusabalalisa okuqukethwe okulimazayo, ukweqa imikhawulo yobuchwepheshe, ukuphazamisa uhlelo lokusebenza, ukuhlaziya kabusha indlela olwakhiwe ngayo, ukuluhlakaza, ukuluguqula, ukuluthengisa kabusha noma ukusabalalisa amakhophi alo aguquliwe, ngaphandle uma lokho kuvunyelwe ngumthetho osebenzayo.

8. Imikhawulo yokusetshenziswa ezimweni ezibucayi

I-ShiftAlarm ayihloselwe ukusetshenziswa njengethuluzi elilodwa kuphela ezimweni lapho iphutha, ukubambezeleka, ukungaqali kwesenzakalo, ukubala okungalungile noma ukulahleka kwedatha kungalimaza ukuphila, impilo, ukuphepha, impahla, ezokuthutha, imishini yezimboni, amadivayisi ezokwelapha, amasistimu ezokuphepha, izinsizakalo zezimo eziphuthumayo, izikhathi ezinqunyiwe zomthetho, imiphumela yomsebenzi noma kubangele ukulahleka kwezimali okukhulu.

Ngamashifu abalulekile, uhambo, izinqubo zezokwelapha, izivivinyo, izindiza, izikhathi ezinqunyiwe zomthetho kanye neminye imicimbi ebalulekile, umsebenzisi kufanele asebenzise ukuhlola nezikhumbuzi ezengeziwe ezizimele.

9. Izinsizakalo nezingxenye zezinhlangano zangaphandle

Kwezinye izici, uhlelo lokusebenza lungasebenzisana nezinsizakalo nezingxenye zesistimu zezinhlangano zangaphandle, okuhlanganisa izitolo zezinhlelo zokusebenza, i-GitHub noma eminye imithombo ekude yokulanda amaphakheji ezilimi, amadokhumenti omthetho, izinto zosizo, imiyalelo namamodeli okuguqula inkulumo abe umbhalo angaxhunyiwe ku-inthanethi; amamephu asekelwe ku-osmdroid/OpenStreetMap; izinsizakalo zokuthola ikheli ngezixhumanisi zendawo; i-Android Geocoder yesistimu; insizakalo yesistimu ye-Android yokubona inkulumo; kanye nezinsizakalo ezijwayelekile zesistimu ye-Android zezaziso, ama-alamu, indawo, amafayela nomsebenzi wangemuva.

Ukusetshenziswa kwezinsizakalo zezinhlangano zangaphandle kulawulwa yimigomo nezinqubomgomo zazo. Umthuthukisi akalawuli izinsizakalo zangaphandle, izitolo zezinhlelo zokusebenza, izinsizakalo zesistimu yedivayisi, amanethiwekhi okuxhumana, amaseva amamephu, izinsizakalo zokubona inkulumo noma izinsizakalo zamafu ezikhethwe ngumsebenzisi.

10. Amakhophi ayisipele

Amakhophi ayisipele adalwa ngesenzo somsebenzisi. Umsebenzisi unesibopho sokudala amakhophi ayisipele, ukugcina amafayela, ukuwavikela ekufinyeleleni okungagunyaziwe, ukuhlola ukuthi ikhophi isafanele futhi isesikhathini nokubuyisela idatha.

Umthuthukisi akaqinisekisi ukubuyiselwa kwedatha efayeleni lekhophi eyisipele elonakele, eliguquliwe, eliphelelwe yisikhathi noma elingatholakali.

11. Impahla yobuhlakani

Igama le-ShiftAlarm, umklamo, izixhumi ezibonakalayo, ikhodi yomthombo, isakhiwo sohlelo lokusebenza, imibhalo, izithombe, indlela yokusebenza, amadokhumenti nezinye izingxenye zohlelo lokusebenza kungokwomthuthukisi noma kusetshenziswa ngokusemthethweni. Umsebenzisi uthola ilungelo elilinganiselwe, elingakhethekile nelingadluliseki lokusebenzisa uhlelo lokusebenza ngokuvumelana nale Migomo nemithetho yesitolo sezinhlelo zokusebenza.

Imitapo yolwazi yomthombo ovulekile, amamephu, amamodeli nezinye izingxenye zezinhlangano zangaphandle zingasatshalaliswa ngaphansi kwemigomo yamalayisensi abanikazi bamalungelo abafanele.

12. Izicelo nezikhalo

Mayelana nokusebenza kohlelo lokusebenza, amadokhumenti, ubumfihlo, ukuthenga, amaphutha nezikhalo, umsebenzisi angaxhumana no-support@insdeco.ru. Kunconywa ukuthi umsebenzisi afake inguqulo yohlelo lokusebenza, imodeli yedivayisi, inguqulo ye-Android, incazelo yenkinga kanye, uma kufanele, nombiko wokuxilonga umsebenzisi awubona kufanele ukwabelana ngawo.

13. Ukuyeka ukusebenzisa

Umsebenzisi angayeka ukusebenzisa uhlelo lokusebenza noma nini ngokulususa kudivayisi noma ngokusula idatha yalo. Ukususa uhlelo lokusebenza noma ukusula idatha kungasusa idatha yasendaweni uma umsebenzisi engazange adale ikhophi eyisipele kusengaphambili.

Umthuthukisi angayeka ukusekela izinguqulo ezindala zohlelo lokusebenza, aguqule ukutholakala kwemisebenzi ethile, abuyekeze amadokhumenti noma ayeke ukusabalalisa uhlelo lokusebenza ezitolo ezithile lapho kudingeka ngenxa yezizathu zobuchwepheshe, zomthetho noma zenhlangano.

14. Izinguquko zamadokhumenti

Umthuthukisi angabuyekeza le Migomo, Inqubomgomo yobumfihlo, Isitatimende sokuzihlangula nezinto zosizo. Izibuyekezo zingalethwa nesibuyekezo sohlelo lokusebenza, zilandwe emthonjeni okude noma zivulwe enguqulweni eyisipele efakwe ngaphakathi kohlelo lokusebenza.

Ngezinguquko ezibalulekile, uhlelo lokusebenza lungacela umsebenzisi ukuthi aphinde abuyekeze noma amukele amadokhumenti abuyekeziwe. Ukuqhubeka nokusebenzisa uhlelo lokusebenza ngemva kokuthi izinguquko seziqalile ukusebenza kusho ukwamukela imigomo ebuyekeziwe, ngaphandle uma umthetho osebenzayo udinga enye inqubo.

15. Umthetho osebenzayo nezinhlinzeko zokugcina

Amadokhumenti alungiselelwe ukusetshenziswa kohlelo lokusebenza emhlabeni wonke futhi kufanele ahunyushwe kucatshangelwa imithetho ebophezelayo esebenzayo, imithetho yezitolo zezinhlelo zokusebenza, izidingo zokuvikelwa kwabathengi kanye nezidingo zokuvikelwa kwedatha ezisebenza kumsebenzisi noma kumthuthukisi. Akukho kulawa madokhumenti okunciphisa amalungelo omsebenzisi angenakuncishiswa ngaphansi kwemithetho ebophezelayo yezwe lomsebenzisi.

Uma noma yisiphi isinhlinzeko sithathwa njengesingavumelekile noma esingaphoqeleleki, ezinye izinhlinzeko ziyaqhubeka nokusebenza. Izinguqulo zamadokhumenti zesiRashiya nesiNgisi zihloselwe injongo efanayo; uma kukhona ukungqubuzana, inguqulo edingwa ngumthetho osebenzayo noma imithetho yenkundla iyona ehamba phambili, futhi uma singekho leso sidingo, inguqulo yesiRashiya iyona ehamba phambili.
