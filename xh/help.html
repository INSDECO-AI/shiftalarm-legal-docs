Isikhokelo somsebenzisi se-ShiftAlarm

Inguqulelo 1. Umhla: 2026-06-23.

1. Ukuqalisa okokuqala

Vula usetyenziso, khetha ulwimi olufunekayo, jonga iimvume uze usete ukuthembeka kwee-alam. Kwezinye izixhobo, kusenokufuneka uvumele umsebenzi wangasemva kwaye ususe imida yebhetri.

2. Ii-alam

Yenza ii-alam eziqhelekileyo, ii-alam eziphindaphindwayo ngeentsuku, ii-alam zomhla kunye nee-alam ezidityaniswe neeshifti. Kwii-alam ezibalulekileyo, jonga izaziso, ii-alam ezichanekileyo kunye nemida yebhetri.

3. Ii-alam zendawo

Yenza i-alam yendawo emephini ngokucofa ixesha elide. Ukuze ithembeke ngakumbi, sebenzisa iradiyasi eyi-800–1000 m uze uvumele indawo echanekileyo nendawo yangasemva.

4. Iishifti

Yenza ishedyuli yeeshifti esebenzayo. Ii-alam ezidityaniswe neeshifti kunye neewijethi zekhalenda zisebenzisa ishedyuli esebenzayo kwaye zithathela ingqalelo utshintsho lweentsuku.

5. Amanqaku nezikhumbuzo

Sebenzisa amanqaku okubhaliweyo, uluhlu, iziganeko, izikhumbuzo, amanqaku esandi kunye nombhalo welizwi. Iimodeli zentetho ezingasebenzisi intanethi zinokukhutshelwa kuSeto.

6. Iiwijethi

Yongeza iWijethi yanamhlanje okanye iWijethi yekhalenda kwisikrini sasekhaya se-Android. Iiwijethi zibonisa ulwazi olusondeleyo kwaye zihlaziywa ngokuzenzekelayo; kukho neqhosha lokuhlaziya ngesandla.

7. Iikopi zogcino

Useto luvumela ukugcina ikopi yogcino efowunini okanye ukwabelana ngayo ngephepha lokwabelana le-Android.

8. Uxilongo

Ukuba umsebenzi awusebenzi kakuhle, vula ilogi yoxilongo ehambelanayo, uyikopishe okanye uyithumele kumphuhlisi kunye nenkcazelo yengxaki.
