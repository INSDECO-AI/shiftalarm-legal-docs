Isilandulo se-ShiftAlarm

Inguqulelo 2. Umhla: 2026-06-29.

I-ShiftAlarm lusetyenziso oluncedisayo lokulungiselela iishifti, ii-alam, ii-alam zendawo, amanqaku, izikhumbuzo, isibali-xesha, isitophuwotshi, iiwijethi, iikopi zogcino kunye nemisebenzi enxulumene noko. Usetyenziso alusiyonkqubo eqinisekisiweyo yokhuseleko, yesilumkiso songxamiseko, yokubeka esweni ezonyango, ye-HR, yocwangciso-mali okanye yokuhlawulwa kwemivuzo.

1. Usetyenziso lunikezelwa “njengoko lunjalo”

Usetyenziso lunikezelwa “njengoko lunjalo” kwaye “njengoko lufumaneka”. Umphuhlisi uthatha amanyathelo afanelekileyo ukugcina usetyenziso luzinzile, kodwa akaqinisekisi ukuba yonke imisebenzi iya kusebenza ngaphandle kweempazamo, ukulibaziseka, iziganeko eziphosiweyo, ukungahambelani, ukulahleka kwedatha okanye imida kuzo zonke izixhobo, iinguqulelo ze-Android, i-firmware yomenzi kunye neemeko zokusetyenziswa.

2. Akukho siqinisekiso sipheleleyo sokuhanjiswa

Ii-alam, ii-alam ezidityaniswe neeshifti, ii-alam zendawo, izibali-xesha, izikhumbuzo, izaziso kunye neewijethi zixhomekeke kwi-Android, kumenzi wesixhobo, kwiimvume, kwixesha lenkqubo elichanekileyo, kwimowudi yokonga ibhetri, kwimida yomsebenzi wangasemva, kwimeko yebhetri, kwimowudi ethi Do Not Disturb, kuseto lwezaziso, ekuqalisweni kwakhona, kuhlaziyo lwenkqubo yokusebenza, ekuvalweni kosetyenziso ngesandla, kwiimpazamo zenkqubo nakwizenzo zomsebenzisi.

Ukuhanjiswa kunokulibaziseka, kuphoswe, kwenzeke ngexesha elahlukileyo kunelilindelweyo, kwenzeke ngaphandle kwesandi okanye kwesaziso, okanye kuthintelwe yinkqubo.

3. Iziganeko ezibalulekileyo nokusetyenziswa okubalulekileyo

Musa ukusebenzisa i-ShiftAlarm njengeyona ndlela kuphela yokulawula iziganeko ezibalulekileyo. Kwiishifti ezibalulekileyo, uhambo, iinkqubo zonyango, iimviwo, iinqwelomoya, imihla yokugqibela esemthethweni, iziganeko ezibalulekileyo ngokwemali kunye neminye imisebenzi ebalulekileyo, sebenzisa izikhumbuzo nohlolo olongezelelweyo oluzimeleyo.

Usetyenziso akufuneki lusetyenziswe njengenxalenye yeenkqubo apho impazamo okanye ukulibaziseka kunokubangela umonakalo kubomi, impilo, ukhuseleko, impahla, ezothutho, izixhobo zemizi-mveliso, izixhobo zonyango, iinkqubo zokhuseleko okanye ukuphendula kwimeko yongxamiseko.

4. Ii-alam zendawo nendawo

Ii-alam zendawo zixhomekeke kwi-GPS, kwi-Wi-Fi, kuthungelwano lweselula, ekuchanekeni kwendawo, ekusetyenzisweni ngaphakathi, ekuxineneni kwezakhiwo, kwiimeko zemozulu, kwindawo yangasemva, kwimida yenkqubo ye-Android, kuseto lwebhetri, kwiradiyasi ye-geofence, kumgangatho wemephu nakufumaneko lweenkonzo zangaphandle.

I-alam yendawo inokuqalisa emva kwexesha, ingaqali, iqale ngokungachanekanga okanye ithintelwe yinkqubo. Ii-alam zendawo azenzelwanga ukulandelela abantu ngokufihlakeleyo, ukubeka esweni abasebenzi, imisebenzi yokhuseleko, izaziso zongxamiseko okanye imisebenzi apho impazamo yendawo inokubangela umonakalo.

5. Iimephu needilesi

Iimephu, iidilesi, iikhawuntinethi kunye neziphumo zokufumana idilesi ngokusekelwe kwiikhawuntinethi zinokungapheleli, ziphelelwe lixesha, zingachaneki okanye zingafumaneki. Idilesi efunyenwe kwiikhawuntinethi lulwazi oluncedisayo kwaye ayisosiqinisekiso sendawo esichanekileyo ngokwasemthethweni.

6. Isandi nemibhalo eguqulwe kwintetho

Ukuqaphela intetho, kuquka ukuguqulela intetho ngaphandle kweintanethi nakwi-intanethi, kunokuquka iimpazamo, okushiyiweyo, ukuqondwa ngokungachanekanga kwamagama, amanani, imihla, amagama obugcisa, iilwimi namabinzana. Umsebenzisi kufuneka azijonge ngokuzimeleyo iirekhodi ezibalulekileyo.

Ukurekhodwa kwesandi nemibhalo eguqulwe kwintetho akufuneki kusetyenziswe njengomthombo kuphela wolwazi olubalulekileyo ngokwasemthethweni, lwezonyango, lwezemali, lwe-HR okanye olunye ulwazi olubalulekileyo.

7. Umvuzo nezibalo

Imisebenzi yomvuzo, iibhonasi, ukutsalwa, izohlwayo, umlinganiselo weshifti kunye neengxelo yeyoncedo nolwazi kuphela. Ayisosiluleko socwangciso-mali, serhafu, sezomthetho okanye se-HR.

Umsebenzisi kufuneka aziqinisekise ngokuzimeleyo izibalo ngokuthelekisa namaxwebhu asemthethweni omqeshi, izivumelwano zengqesho, iziliphu zomvuzo, imigaqo yerhafu kunye nomthetho osebenzayo.

8. Iikopi zogcino nokulahleka kwedatha

Umphuhlisi akaqinisekisi ukugcinwa kwedatha ukuba umsebenzisi akazange enze ikopi yogcino, ulahlekelwe yifayile, uyigcine kwindawo engafumanekiyo, ucime idatha yosetyenziso, acime ugcino, atshintshe izixhobo, asebenzise ifayile eyonakeleyo, acime iimvume ezifunekayo okanye abelane ngefayile namaqela esithathu.

Iikopi zogcino zinokuquka ulwazi lobuqu. Umsebenzisi unoxanduva lokuzigcina, ukuzidlulisa, ukuzikhusela nokuzicima.

9. Iinkonzo zamaqela esithathu

Eminye imisebenzi inokuxhomekeka kwiivenkile zosetyenziso, kwi-GitHub okanye kweminye imithombo ekude, kwi-OpenStreetMap/osmdroid, kwiinkonzo zokufumana idilesi, kwinkonzo yenkqubo ye-Android yokuqaphela intetho, kwiinkonzo ze-imeyile, kugcino lwelifu, kwinkqubo yokusebenza nakumenzi wesixhobo.

Umphuhlisi akanoxanduva lokufumaneka, ukuchaneka, ukhuseleko, imigaqo, utshintsho, ukusilela, imida okanye izenzo zeenkonzo zamaqela esithathu ezikhethwe ngumsebenzisi okanye ezinikezelwa yinkqubo yokusebenza.

10. Izenzo zomsebenzisi

Umsebenzisi unoxanduva lokuseta ngokuchanekileyo usetyenziso, iishedyuli, ii-alam, ii-geofence, iimvume, izaziso, ibhetri, umsebenzi wangasemva, iikopi zogcino, ixesha lenkqubo kunye nokujonga ukuthembeka kwemisebenzi kwisixhobo sakhe.

11. Umda woxanduva

Ngowona mda uphezulu uvunyelwe ngumthetho osebenzayo, umphuhlisi akanoxanduva lomonakalo ongathanga ngqo, inzuzo elahlekileyo, izohlwayo, iziphumo zoqeqesho emsebenzini, iishifti eziphosiweyo, ukufika emva kwexesha, ilahleko yemali, ukulahleka kwedatha, izibalo ezingachanekanga, iimpazamo zomsebenzisi, ukusilela kwesixhobo, imida ye-Android, izenzo zabavelisi bezixhobo, uthungelwano lonxibelelwano, iivenkile zosetyenziso okanye iinkonzo zamaqela esithathu.

Akukho nto kolu xwebhu enciphisa amalungelo omsebenzisi angenakuncitshiswa phantsi komthetho osebenzayo osisinyanzelo.

12. Amaxwebhu anxulumene noko

Esi Silandulo sisebenza kunye neMigaqo yokusetyenziswa ye-ShiftAlarm kunye noMgaqo-nkqubo wabucala. Ukuba umsebenzisi akavumelani nale mida nale mingcipheko, kufuneka ayeke ukusebenzisa usetyenziso.
