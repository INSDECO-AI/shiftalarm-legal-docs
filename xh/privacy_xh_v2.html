Umgaqo-nkqubo wabucala we-ShiftAlarm

Inguqulelo 2. Umhla: 2026-06-29.

1. Umgaqo ngokubanzi wokuqhubekekiswa kwedatha

I-ShiftAlarm isebenza ikakhulu ngendlela ethi offline-first. Idatha ephambili yomsebenzisi yenziwa, iqhubekekiswe kwaye igcinwe kwisixhobo somsebenzisi. Umphuhlisi akayifumani ngokuzenzekelayo loo datha ngaphandle kokuba umsebenzisi uyithumela ngesicelo senkxaso, ngokukhuphela ngaphandle, nge-imeyile, ngomthunywa, ngogcino lwelifu, ngevenkile yosetyenziso okanye ngenye indlela ekhethwe ngumsebenzisi.

Kule nguqulelo, usetyenziso alusebenzisi uthungelwano lwentengiso, inkqubo yalo yeeakhawunti, ugunyaziso kwiseva, i-Firebase Analytics, i-Google Analytics, i-Crashlytics okanye i-AdMob, ngaphandle kokuba oku kuchazwe ngokucacileyo kwinguqulelo ehlaziyiweyo yalo Mgaqo-nkqubo.

2. Idatha enokugcinwa kwisixhobo

Kuxhomekeke kwimisebenzi esetyenzisiweyo, usetyenziso lunokugcina le datha ilandelayo kwisixhobo:

  • useto losetyenziso, ulwimi, umxholo, useto lweewijethi kunye nokhetho lomsebenzisi;
  • ii-alam eziqhelekileyo, ii-alam ezidityaniswe neeshifti, ixesha, uphindaphindo, izandi, ukungcangcazela, ukulibazisa, ukunyuka kwesandi ngokuthe ngcembe kunye noseto olunxulumene noko;
  • iishedyuli zeeshifti, iitemplate zeeshifti, utshintsho lwekhalenda, iintsuku zokuphumla kunye namanqaku omsebenzisi;
  • ii-alam zendawo: iikhawuntinethi, iradiyasi, uhlobo lwesiganeko sokungena okanye ukuphuma, idilesi, igama lendawo, iindawo ezigciniweyo, ishedyuli kunye nemeko yokuqalisa;
  • amanqaku, iziganeko, izikhumbuzo, uluhlu lokukhangela, iibhloko zombhalo kunye nemihla enxulumene nazo;
  • amanqaku esandi, ukurekhodwa kwelizwi kunye nemibhalo eguqulwe kwintetho;
  • isibali-xesha, isitophuwotshi, iiwotshi zehlabathi kunye nembali yezinye izenzo xa loo mbali iyinxalenye yomsebenzi;
  • useto lwezibalo zomvuzo, iibhonasi, ukutsalwa, izohlwayo, iingxelo kunye nezibalo ezinxulumene noko;
  • iilogi zoxilongo, iziganeko zobugcisa, imeko yeemvume, iimpazamo kunye neemeko zemisebenzi;
  • iikopi zogcino ukuba umsebenzisi uzenza ngesandla.

3. Idatha anokuyithumela umsebenzisi kumphuhlisi

Ukuba umsebenzisi uqhagamshelana nenkxaso ku-support@insdeco.ru, umphuhlisi unokufumana idilesi ye-imeyile yomsebenzisi, igama okanye utyikityo lwe-imeyile, umbhalo womyalezo, iziqhoboshelo, imifanekiso yesikrini, iifayile zogcino, iingxelo zoxilongo, imodeli yesixhobo, inguqulelo ye-Android, inguqulelo yosetyenziso, ixesha lesiganeko kunye nenkcazelo yengxaki.

Loo datha ithunyelwa kuphela ngesenzo somsebenzisi. Umsebenzisi kufuneka ahlole i-imeyile neziqhoboshelo ngaphambi kokuzithumela.

4. Iinjongo zokuqhubekekiswa

Idatha isetyenziselwa ukuqhuba imisebenzi yosetyenziso, ukugcina useto lomsebenzisi, ukuhambisa ii-alam nezikhumbuzo, ukuqhuba ii-alam zendawo, ukubonisa amanqaku neewijethi, ukwenza iikopi zogcino, ukuxilonga iimpazamo, ukuqhubekekisa izicelo zenkxaso, ukuphucula uzinzo losetyenziso, ukuthobela iimfuno zomthetho kunye nokukhusela amalungelo omphuhlisi nawomsebenzisi.

Kuxhomekeke kumthetho osebenzayo, iziseko zomthetho zokuqhubekekiswa zinokuquka ukuphunyezwa kwesivumelwano nomsebenzisi, isenzo somsebenzisi, imvume yeemvume ezithile, umdla osemthethweni kwinkxaso nokhuseleko, kunye nokuthobela uxanduva lomthetho.

5. Iimvume zesixhobo

Usetyenziso lunokucela kuphela iimvume ezifunekayo kwimisebenzi efanelekileyo:

  • izaziso — zee-alam, izibali-xesha, izikhumbuzo kunye nezaziso zenkonzo;
  • ii-alam ezichanekileyo neemvume zee-alam — ukuze ziqale ngexesha elikhethwe ngumsebenzisi;
  • indawo ngexesha lokusebenzisa usetyenziso — ukukhetha iindawo, iimephu nokuseta ii-alam zendawo;
  • indawo yangasemva — yee-alam zendawo xa usetyenziso lungavulwanga;
  • imakrofoni — yamanqaku esandi, igalelo lelizwi nokuqaphela intetho;
  • i-intanethi — yokukhuphela iipakethe zolwimi, amaxwebhu asemthethweni, uncedo, imiyalelo, iimodeli zokuguqulela intetho ngaphandle kweintanethi, iimephu kunye nemisebenzi yenethiwekhi;
  • ukufikelela kwiifayile ngesikhethi seefayile senkqubo — ukukhuphela ngaphandle nokungenisa ugcino;
  • useto lwebhetri nomsebenzi wangasemva — ukuphucula ukuthembeka kwee-alam, izaziso nee-alam zendawo.

Umsebenzisi unokurhoxisa iimvume kuSeto lwe-Android. Emva kokurhoxiswa, eminye imisebenzi inokungafumaneki okanye isebenze ngemida.

6. Indawo neemephu

Idatha yendawo isetyenziselwa ukukhetha indawo emephini, ukugcina ii-alam zendawo, ukubona ukungena okanye ukuphuma kwindawo echaziweyo, ukubonisa idilesi nokwenza iindawo ezigciniweyo. Iikhawuntinethi noseto lwee-alam zendawo zigcinwa kwisixhobo ngaphandle kokuba umsebenzisi uzikhuphela ngaphandle okanye azithumele.

Xa umsebenzisi esebenzisa iimephu okanye ukukhangela idilesi, usetyenziso lunokuqhagamshelana namacandelo eemephu nawokufumana idilesi, kuquka i-osmdroid/OpenStreetMap, i-Nominatim reverse geocoding, i-Android Geocoder yenkqubo okanye enye inkonzo efumanekayo kwisixhobo. Ezi nkonzo zinokufumana idatha yesicelo sobugcisa, idilesi ye-IP, iikhawuntinethi okanye enye idatha efunekayo ukubonisa imephu okanye ukufumana idilesi.

7. Isandi, ukuguqulela intetho ngaphandle kweintanethi nakwi-intanethi

Amanqaku esandi nemibhalo eguqulwe kwintetho yenziwa ngesenzo somsebenzisi kwaye inokuquka ulwazi lobuqu okanye olubuthathaka ukuba umsebenzisi ulurekhoda. Igcinwa kwisixhobo ngaphandle kokuba umsebenzisi uyikhuphela ngaphandle okanye ayithumele.

Ukuguqulela intetho ngaphandle kweintanethi kunokusebenzisa iimodeli zokuqaphela intetho ezikhutshelwe kwisixhobo. Xa ezi modeli zikhutshelwa, iseva ekude inokubona idatha yobugcisa yoqhagamshelo efana nedilesi ye-IP kunye nolwazi lwesicelo.

Ukuqaphela intetho kwi-intanethi kunokusebenzisa inkonzo yenkqubo ye-Android yokuqaphela intetho ekhethwe sisixhobo, umenzi, umsebenzisi okanye inkqubo yokusebenza. Kule meko, idatha yesandi nolwazi lobugcisa lunokuthunyelwa kwaye luqhubekekiswe yinkonzo ekhethiweyo yokuqaphela intetho phantsi kwemigaqo yayo. Umphuhlisi we-ShiftAlarm akayilawuli imigaqo-nkqubo yaloo nkonzo yenkqubo.

8. Ukukhuphela ngenethiwekhi nemithombo ekude

Usetyenziso lunokusebenzisa i-intanethi ukukhuphela iipakethe zolwimi, iifayile ze-manifest, amaxwebhu asemthethweni, izixhobo zoncedo, imiyalelo kunye neemodeli zokuguqulela intetho ngaphandle kweintanethi, kuquka kwi-GitHub okanye komnye umthombo okude ochazwe kusetyenziso. Ngexesha lezo zicelo, iseva ekude inokufumana idilesi ye-IP, umhla nexesha lesicelo, indlela yefayile, ulwazi loqhagamshelo kunye nenye idatha yobugcisa eqhelekileyo.

Iifayile ezinokuhlolwa ukuthembeka kwazo kusetyenziso zinokujongwa nge-SHA-256 okanye elinye ixabiso lokukhangela. Oku kunciphisa umngcipheko wokonakala kwefayile kodwa akuqinisekisi ukufumaneka komthombo okude.

9. Iivenkile zosetyenziso neentlawulo

Usetyenziso luthengwa ngevenkile yosetyenziso, njenge-RuStore okanye i-Google Play, ukuba luyafumaneka kwiqonga elifanelekileyo. Ivenkile yosetyenziso iqhubekekisa ngokuzimeleyo iakhawunti yomsebenzisi, iintlawulo, ukubuyiselwa kwemali, imbali yokuthenga, ukufakwa kunye nohlaziyo phantsi kwemigaqo yayo noMgaqo-nkqubo wayo wabucala.

Umphuhlisi akafumani amakhadi ebhanki, amagama okugqitha okuhlawula okanye iinkcukacha ezipheleleyo zokuhlawula xa intlawulo yenziwa ngevenkile yosetyenziso.

10. Iikopi zogcino nokukhuphela ngaphandle

Iikopi zogcino zenziwa kuphela ngesenzo somsebenzisi. Ifayile yogcino inokuquka inxalenye enkulu yedatha yasekuhlaleni yosetyenziso, kuquka ii-alam, iishedyuli, amanqaku, iindawo zejografi, useto, uxilongo kunye nolunye ulwazi.

Ukuba umsebenzisi ugcina okanye abelane ngekopi yogcino ngolunye usetyenziso, ugcino lwelifu, i-imeyile, umthunywa, umphathi weefayile okanye ugcino lwangaphandle, ukhuseleko nokuqhubekekiswa okulandelayo kwaloo fayile kuxhomekeke kwindlela yogcino okanye yokudlulisa ekhethwe ngumsebenzisi.

11. Uxilongo

Iilogi zoxilongo zenzelwe ukusombulula iingxaki nokujonga imisebenzi. Zinokuquka ulwazi lobugcisa malunga neziganeko zosetyenziso, ixesha, iimvume, isixhobo, inguqulelo yenkqubo yokusebenza, inguqulelo yosetyenziso, imeko ye-alam, imeko ye-alam yendawo, amanqaku, iikopi zogcino kunye neempazamo.

Uxilongo lugcinwa kwisixhobo de umsebenzisi alucime okanye aluthumele ngesandla kumphuhlisi.

12. Ukwabelana namaqela esithathu

Umphuhlisi akathengisi idatha yomsebenzisi kubathengisi kwaye akayidluliseli ngokuzenzekelayo idatha yasekuhlaleni yosetyenziso kumaqela esithathu. Idatha inokudluliselwa ukuba umsebenzisi usebenzisa iinkonzo zangaphandle, uthumela isicelo senkxaso, ukhuphela ngaphandle ikopi yogcino, usebenzisa iimephu, ukufumana idilesi, ukuqaphela intetho kwi-intanethi, ivenkile yosetyenziso okanye enye inkonzo yangaphandle.

Iinkonzo zamaqela esithathu ziqhubekekisa idatha phantsi kwemigaqo yazo. Kuyacetyiswa ukuba umsebenzisi ahlole imigaqo-nkqubo yevenkile yosetyenziso ekhethiweyo, inkonzo ye-imeyile, ugcino lwelifu, inkonzo yeemephu, inkonzo yokuqaphela intetho kunye nezinye iinkonzo zangaphandle.

13. Ixesha lokugcinwa kwedatha

Idatha yasekuhlaleni igcinwa kwisixhobo de umsebenzisi ayicime kusetyenziso, acime idatha yosetyenziso, akhuphe usetyenziso, abuyisele idatha kwenye ikopi yogcino okanye acime ifayile yogcino kwindawo ekhethiweyo yokugcina.

Izicelo zenkxaso, ii-imeyile, iziqhoboshelo kunye nezixhobo zoxilongo ezithunyelwe ngumsebenzisi kumphuhlisi zinokugcinwa ngaphandle kwexesha lokuphelelwa elichazwe kwangaphambili ukuze kugcinwe imbali yenkxaso, kuhlalutywe iingxaki eziphindaphindayo, kukhuselwe amalungelo kwaye kuthotyelwe uxanduva lomthetho. Umsebenzisi unokucela ukucinywa kwaloo datha; isicelo siya kuzalisekiswa ngaphandle kokuba ukugcinwa ngakumbi kufunwa okanye kuvunyelwe ngumthetho osebenzayo.

14. Ukhuseleko

Usetyenziso lusebenzisa iindlela eziqhelekileyo ze-Android, kuquka ukwahlulwa kwedatha yosetyenziso yinkqubo yokusebenza, iimvume zenkqubo kunye nesikhethi seefayile senkqubo. Ezinye iifayile ezikhutshelweyo zinokujongwa ngexabiso lokukhangela ukuba olo hlolo luphunyeziwe kolo hlobo lwefayile.

Umsebenzisi unoxanduva lokukhusela isixhobo, isitshixo sesikrini, ukufikelela kwi-imeyile, iinkonzo zelifu, iikopi zogcino kunye nogcino lwangaphandle. Iikopi zogcino neefayile ezikhutshelwe ngaphandle zinokufikeleleka kwabanye abantu ukuba umsebenzisi uzigcina kwindawo engakhuselekanga okanye abelane ngazo namaqela esithathu.

15. Amazwe okuqhubekekiswa

Idatha yasekuhlaleni ihlala kwisixhobo somsebenzisi kwilizwe apho isixhobo sisetyenziswa khona. Idatha edluliselwa ngeevenkile zosetyenziso, i-GitHub, iinkonzo zeemephu, iinkonzo zokuqaphela intetho, iinkonzo ze-imeyile, iinkonzo zelifu kunye nezinye iinkonzo zangaphandle inokucutshungulwa kumazwe apho ezo nkonzo zisebenza khona okanye zigcina izibonelelo zazo.

Idatha ethunyelwa kwinkxaso yomphuhlisi inokucutshungulwa apho umphuhlisi kunye neenkonzo zakhe ze-imeyile, zokusingatha okanye zogcino zikhoyo.

16. Amalungelo omsebenzisi

Umsebenzisi unokujonga, atshintshe kwaye acime uninzi lwedatha ngqo kusetyenziso. Umsebenzisi unokucima idatha yosetyenziso nge-Android, akhuphe usetyenziso, arhoxise iimvume, akhuphele ngaphandle ikopi yogcino okanye acime iifayile zogcino azenzileyo.

Ngokuphathelele idatha ethunyelwe kumphuhlisi, umsebenzisi unokuqhagamshelana no-support@insdeco.ru aze acele ukufikelela, ukulungiswa, ukucinywa, ukuthintelwa kokuqhubekekiswa okanye ukurhoxiswa kwemvume apho la malungelo esebenza ngokomthetho. Ukukhusela idatha, umphuhlisi unokucela ulwazi olufunekayo ukuqinisekisa ukuba isicelo sinxulumene nomsebenzisi okanye nedilesi ye-imeyile efanelekileyo.

Ukuba umsebenzisi ukwindawo enika ilungelo lokufaka isikhalazo kwigunya lokhuseleko lwedatha, unokuqhagamshelana nelo gunya phantsi komthetho osebenzayo.

17. Abantwana

Usetyenziso lunokuba nomlinganiselo wobudala obungu-0+, kodwa alujoliswanga ekuqokeleleni idatha yobuqu yabantwana ngumphuhlisi. Ukuba umntwana usebenzisa usetyenziso, umzali okanye umgcini osemthethweni kufuneka ajonge useto, iimvume, amanqaku, ukurekhodwa kwesandi, iindawo zejografi, iikopi zogcino kunye nezicelo zenkxaso apho oko kufunwa ngumthetho okanye yimigaqo yosapho.

18. Utshintsho kulo Mgaqo-nkqubo

Umphuhlisi unokuhlaziya lo Mgaqo-nkqubo. Inguqulelo yangoku inokuhanjiswa kunye nosetyenziso, ikhutshelwe kumthombo okude okanye ivulwe kwinguqulelo yokubuyela umva efakwe ngaphakathi. Kutshintsho olubalulekileyo, usetyenziso lunokucela umsebenzisi ukuba ahlole okanye amkele uxwebhu oluhlaziyiweyo ukuba oko kufunwa ngumthetho okanye yingqiqo yosetyenziso.
